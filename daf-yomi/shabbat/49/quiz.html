Shabbat 49 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
What question does the Gemara leave unresolved regarding materials like straw and grass that are forbidden for insulation 'when moist'?
A. Whether they may ever be used at all
B. Whether the prohibition applies only when they are naturally moist, or also when moistened by an outside source
C. Whether they must be dried before Shabbat
D. Whether they can insulate cold food
Correct: B — The Gemara raises but cannot conclusively resolve whether 'moist' in the mishna refers only to materials moist by their own nature or also to those wetted by another cause.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why did Elisha claim to the Roman official that the tefillin in his hand were 'doves' wings'?
A. He was joking with the official
B. Rome had decreed death for anyone found wearing tefillin, so he removed them and disguised his claim to escape execution — and a miracle made them actually turn into feathers
C. Tefillin were made of feathers in his time
D. He had forgotten what tefillin were called
Correct: B — Under Roman persecution wearing tefillin was a capital offense; Elisha's claim was validated by a miracle, giving him the name 'Elisha, Man of Wings.'
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to the resolution reached in the Gemara, which material is the subject of Rabbi Yehuda's fine-versus-coarse dispute?
A. Carpenter's wood shavings
B. Flax chaff (ne'oret), since a baraita states that fine flax chaff has the legal status of manure
C. Wool fleece
D. Animal hides
Correct: B — A baraita proves Rabbi Yehuda's statement concerns flax chaff, teaching that the fine variety generates heat like manure and is therefore forbidden, unlike the coarse variety.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
According to the Rabbis (against Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya), how does one remove a pot from a basket insulated with wool fleece?
A. One must tip the basket on its side first
B. One may simply lift out the pot directly, and may also replace it afterward
C. The fleece must be removed entirely beforehand
D. It is forbidden to remove the pot on Shabbat at all
Correct: B — The Rabbis disagree with Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya's more cautious method and hold that one may simply take the pot out and put it back without special precaution.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to Rabbi Chanina bar Chama, why are there exactly thirty-nine primary categories of forbidden labor on Shabbat?
A. They correspond to the number of days in creation
B. They correspond to the specific labors that were performed in constructing the Tabernacle (Mishkan)
C. They correspond to the number of tribes of Israel
D. There is no reason given
Correct: B — Rabbi Chanina bar Chama teaches that the 39 categories of Shabbat labor all have their source in the actual work performed in building the Tabernacle.
