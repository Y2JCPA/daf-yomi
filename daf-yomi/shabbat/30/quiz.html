Shabbat 30 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Why does the mishna use the word 'exempt' rather than 'permitted' for extinguishing a lamp before a critically ill person?
A. Because it is actually forbidden after the fact
B. Because it truly is permitted ab initio, but the mishna uses 'exempt' for stylistic consistency with the following clause about liability
C. Because the sick person must consent first
D. Because it only applies on Festivals
Correct: B — The Gemara explains that although the halakha is full permission for a life-threatening case, the mishna phrased it as 'exempt' to match the wording of the adjacent clause about liability.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to Rabbi Tanḥum's homily, why is it permitted to extinguish a physical lamp to help a critically ill person?
A. Because lamps are not valuable
B. Because both a lamp and a person's soul are called 'ner,' and it is better to extinguish the lamp of flesh and blood than to endanger the lamp of God (the soul)
C. Because Shabbat laws do not apply to lamps
D. Because Solomon ruled this way explicitly
Correct: B — Rabbi Tanḥum concludes his homily by noting that both a physical lamp and a human soul are called 'ner,' so preserving the soul takes precedence over preserving the physical lamp.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to the Gemara's account, what finally caused the Temple gates to open for the Ark during Solomon's dedication?
A. Solomon offering more sacrifices
B. Solomon saying 'remember the good deeds of David Your servant'
C. The Ark being carried by priests instead of Levites
D. Twenty-four days of silence
Correct: B — After Solomon's earlier prayers were unanswered, the gates opened only once he invoked David's merit, at which point fire descended and Israel knew David had been forgiven.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why did the Sages nearly suppress the Book of Ecclesiastes?
A. Because it was written too late
B. Because its statements appeared to contradict each other
C. Because it never mentions God
D. Because Solomon was considered unreliable
Correct: B — The Gemara states the Sages considered suppressing Ecclesiastes because of its apparently self-contradictory statements, but kept it since its beginning and end are firmly rooted in Torah.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What baraita does the daf conclude with, setting up the following stories?
A. A person should always study alone
B. A person should always be patient like Hillel and not impatient like Shammai
C. A person should never argue about halakha
D. A person should follow Rabban Gamliel in all matters
Correct: B — The daf ends with the Sages' teaching that one should emulate Hillel's patience rather than Shammai's impatience, introducing an incident involving two people that continues onto the next daf.
