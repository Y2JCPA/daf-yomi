📖 Overview: Standing Before God, Sitting in Judgment

Shabbat 10a–10b
הִכּוֹן לִקְרַאת אֱלֹהֶיךָ יִשְׂרָאֵל
The scene: The daf opens mid-discussion of preparing oneself with dignity before prayer, quoting Amos: 'Prepare to greet your God, Israel.' From there the Gemara moves, by association, through a set of independent teachings: whether Torah study may be interrupted for prayer, when a court session formally begins and how long judges may sit before eating, where in a bathhouse one may pray or greet a friend, and why a gift-giver — God included, with the gift of Shabbat — must inform the recipient that a gift has been given. The daf closes with practical wisdom from Rav about family and where to live.

⏰ Court in Session: From Opening to the Judges' Meal

Rabbi Yirmeya, Rabbi Yona, Rav Sheshet, and a baraita on daily eating times
QuestionAnswer
When does a trial begin?Rabbi Yirmeya: from when the judges wrap themselves in their prayer shawls. Rabbi Yona: from when the litigants begin to plead — not a real dispute, since it depends on whether the court was already sitting
Until when do judges sit?Rav Sheshet: until mealtime, since 'your ministers eat... in strength and not in drunkenness' (Ecclesiastes 10:16–17) — i.e. only after Torah and judgment
Who eats at which hour?1st hour: Ludim (ravenous cannibals); 2nd: robbers; 3rd: heirs; 4th: all people; 5th: workers; 6th: Torah scholars, who study until court adjourns
One true hour: Rav Ḥiyya bar Rav from Difti taught: 'the people stood over Moses from the morning until the evening' (Exodus 18:13) cannot mean Moses judged all day — his own Torah study needed time. Rather, any judge who renders one truthful judgment, even for a single hour, is considered a partner with God in the work of Creation (Genesis 1:5, 'evening and morning, one day').

📖 Torah Study or Prayer — Which Takes Priority?

Two sages, each criticized for the opposite excess
Rava
Watching Rav Hamnuna prolong his prayer: 'They abandon eternal life — Torah study — and occupy themselves with temporal life!'
Defense
Rav Hamnuna held that the time for prayer is distinct from the time for Torah; one is not at the expense of the other.
R' Yirmeya
Sitting before Rabbi Zeira engaged in halakha, he hurried to finish as the time for prayer approached.
R' Zeira
Applied to him: מֵסִיר אׇזְנוֹ מִשְּׁמוֹעַ תּוֹרָה גַּם תְּפִלָּתוֹ תּוֹעֵבָה — 'One who turns his ear from hearing Torah, his prayer too is an abomination' (Proverbs 28:9).
Tension: The Gemara juxtaposes both incidents without resolving them — a warning against excess in either direction.

🛁 Where in the Bathhouse May One Pray?

Rav Adda bar Ahava's ruling, tested against a Tosefta's three rooms
❓ Rav Adda bar Ahava: a person may, ab initio, recite his prayer in the bathhouse
↓
📜 A Tosefta describes a Roman bathhouse in three rooms
↓
Outer room
People stand dressed — Torah reading, prayer, greeting, and tefillin all permitted
Middle room
People stand both naked and dressed — greeting permitted; no prayer or reading; tefillin not removed but not donned anew
Inner room
People stand naked — nothing permitted; tefillin must be removed
↓
✅ Resolved: Rav Adda meant only an empty, new bathhouse never yet used for bathing — even there, 'shalom' stays forbidden, since it is one of God's own names (Judges 6:24)

🎁 A Good Gift Locked Away — and Shabbat Is Its Name

Why a giver must announce a gift
מַתָּנָה טוֹבָה יֵשׁ לִי בְּבֵית גְּנָזַי וְשַׁבָּת שְׁמָהּ, וַאֲנִי מְבַקֵּשׁ לִיתְּנָהּ לְיִשְׂרָאֵל, לֵךְ וְהוֹדִיעָם
Rav's teaching: One who gives a gift to another must inform him, derived from Exodus 31:13, 'to know that I am God who sanctifies you' — when God gave Israel the gift of Shabbat, He told Moses: 'I have a good gift in My treasure house and Shabbat is its name; go inform them.' From this Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel derived that one who gives bread to a child must inform the mother — originally by smearing the child with oil, later (once witchcraft was a concern) with the same food given. Rav Ḥisda so prized this teaching that he rewarded Rava bar Meḥasseya with two priestly gifts for reporting it in Rav's name.

👨‍👦 Three More Teachings from Rav

Reported by Rava bar Meḥasseya in the name of Rav Ḥama bar Gurya
1️⃣ Never favor one son over the others — Jacob gave Joseph an extra two sela of fine wool for his coat, his brothers grew jealous, and that chain of events led our ancestors down to Egypt
↓
2️⃣ Always seek to settle in a newly-founded town — fewer years means fewer accumulated sins, as Lot called Zoar 'a small (i.e. recently settled) city' when he fled there (Genesis 19:20)
↓
3️⃣ Rabbi Avin: the numerical value of נָא ('please') in Lot's plea 'let me escape there' is 51 — one year less than Sodom's 52, proving Zoar truly was newer
