Shabbat 51 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
How does the Gemara resolve the contradiction between Rav's prohibition on insulating cold food and the baraita saying Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi permitted it?
A. The two statements refer to different foods
B. Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi retracted his prohibition after hearing that Rabbi Yosei permitted it
C. One statement is a scribal error
D. Rav and Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi never actually disagreed
Correct: B — Rabbi Yishmael, son of Rabbi Yosei, told Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi that his father Rabbi Yosei permitted insulating cold food, and Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi said 'the Elder has already ruled' and deferred to him.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why did Rabbi Ami become angry at Rav Naḥman's instructions to his slave, even though each ruling Rav Naḥman relied on was individually valid?
A. Because gentile-cooked water is always forbidden
B. Because a distinguished person is held to a stricter standard so his conduct is not mistaken for a violation
C. Because insulating cold food is always forbidden
D. Because slaves may not perform any labor on Shabbat
Correct: B — The Gemara explains that Rabbi Ami held 'an important person is different' — such a person should be stringent to avoid the appearance of impropriety.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to Rabban Shimon ben Gamliel, why is it permitted to pour hot water from one urn into another and then insulate the second urn?
A. Because insulation is always permitted
B. Because pouring the water already cooled it, so there is no concern one will reboil it
C. Because urns are never considered muktzeh
D. Because the water was never actually hot
Correct: B — Since the water has already been transferred and thereby cooled somewhat, there is no longer a concern that insulating it will lead to reboiling.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why is it prohibited to crush snow or hail on Shabbat to extract its water, while placing it in a cup to melt on its own is permitted?
A. Crushing is louder and disturbs the peace of Shabbat
B. Crushing actively creates a new entity (water) on Shabbat, while passive melting does not
C. Snow is muktzeh but hail is not
D. There is no difference; both are forbidden
Correct: B — Actively crushing snow or hail to force water out constitutes creating a new substance, a prohibited act, whereas letting it melt on its own is passive and permitted.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to the new mishna beginning the next chapter, with what may a horse go out into the public domain on Shabbat?
A. An afsar (halter)
B. A chatam (nose ring)
C. A perumbiya (iron halter)
D. A shir (chain), and it may even be pulled by it
Correct: D — The mishna states that a horse may go out with a shir (chain) around its neck, and, like all animals that typically wear such chains, may even be pulled by it.
