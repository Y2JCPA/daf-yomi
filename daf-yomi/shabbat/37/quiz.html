Shabbat 37 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
What does the Gemara mean when it says the mishna is 'chaseirah mechusrah' (incomplete)?
A. The mishna has a scribal error that cannot be fixed
B. A clause about leaving a pot on the stove must be inserted for the mishna to make full sense
C. The mishna is missing an entire chapter
D. Beit Shammai's opinion was accidentally omitted
Correct: B — The Gemara resolves the mishna by supplying a missing clause about leaving a pot on the stove, which then sets up the later Beit Shammai/Beit Hillel dispute properly.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to the Tosefta, is it permitted to lean a pot against an unswept, uncovered stove heated with pomace or wood?
A. No, leaning is treated the same as placing inside
B. Yes — only placing food inside the stove requires it to be swept or ashed
C. It is permitted only on Friday, never on Shabbat
D. It is permitted only for hot water, never for cooked food
Correct: B — The Tosefta distinguishes leaning (permitted even unswept) from placing inside (forbidden unswept).
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What does Rav Oshaya permit leaving on a stove that was properly covered with ash before Shabbat but then reignites?
A. Nothing at all, until it is re-covered
B. Any food, regardless of how cooked it is
C. Hot water and food that are already fully heated/cooked
D. Only cold food that has not yet been touched by fire
Correct: C — Rav Oshaya rules that fully-heated water and fully-cooked food may be left, since there is no concern one will stoke the coals for food that needs nothing more.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why does Rav and Shmuel's ruling on 'mitztamek v'yafeh lo' (shrivels and improves) forbid leaving such food on the fire, according to the dispute on this daf?
A. Because it violates the laws of tithing
B. Because there is concern one will stoke the coals to further improve it
C. Because it is not kosher once shriveled
D. Because it must be eaten within an hour of cooking
Correct: B — Food that would benefit from continued heat creates a temptation to stoke the coals, which is why Rav and Shmuel forbid leaving it, though Rabbi Yochanan is more lenient.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to Rav Nachman's rule, which dish shrivels and improves despite containing flour?
A. Porridge
B. Dates cooked in syrup
C. Turnip stew, when it contains meat and is not intended for guests
D. Any dish with fried fish
Correct: C — Rav Nachman singles out turnip stew as the exception to the rule that floury dishes deteriorate — but only when it has meat and is not being prepared for guests.
