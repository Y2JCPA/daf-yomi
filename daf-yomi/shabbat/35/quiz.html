Shabbat 35 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
What is the practical difference between Rabba's and Rav Yosef's measures of twilight?
A. A full mil
B. Half a danka, i.e. one-twelfth of a mil
C. There is no practical difference
D. Exactly one mil and a half
Correct: B — Rabba holds three-quarters of a mil, Rav Yosef two-thirds of a mil; the gap between them is half a danka, or one-twelfth of a mil.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
When the baraita says twilight lasts 'as long as the eastern face of the sky is reddened,' which direction does Rava say one should actually watch?
A. North, toward the pole star
B. The actual eastern horizon
C. West — the face of the sky that causes the east to redden
D. It does not matter; any direction works
Correct: C — Rava explains the phrase describes the cause (the western glow) of the effect (the eastern reddening), so one watches the west, like light through a window landing on the opposite wall.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to Rav Yehuda in the name of Shmuel, what does seeing three (medium-sized) stars indicate?
A. It is still definitely day
B. It is twilight
C. It is night
D. It is exactly midnight
Correct: C — One star means day, two stars mean twilight, and three medium-sized stars mean night has arrived.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
In the sequence of six Friday shofar blasts, what happens after the third blast according to Rabbi Natan?
A. Field workers stop plowing
B. Shops are locked
C. The Shabbat lamp is lit
D. Tefillin are removed
Correct: C — Rabbi Natan holds the third blast signals lighting the Shabbat lamp (Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi instead says it signals removing tefillin).
QUESTION 5 OF 5
Why does Rav Yosef distinguish an individual's shofar (which may be moved on Shabbat) from a community's shofar (which may not)?
A. Individual shofarot are smaller and lighter
B. An individual's shofar has a permitted Shabbat use — giving water to a child through its bent mouth
C. Community property may never be touched on Shabbat
D. Only individuals are obligated to hear the shofar
Correct: B — Rav Yosef holds that because an individual's shofar can be used to pour water for a child, it is not muktze; a communal shofar lacks any such use, so it may not be moved.
