5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
What resolves the apparent tension between forbidding one to remove a saddle and permitting one to place a saddlecloth on Shabbat?
A. Saddles and saddlecloths are treated identically
B. Rav Pappa distinguishes: warming (placing a saddlecloth) is always needed since a donkey feels cold even in summer, while cooling (removing a saddle) involves no suffering
C. Only saddlecloths belong to the animal itself
D. Saddles may only be removed by a gentile
Correct: B — Rav Pappa explains that a saddlecloth addresses genuine suffering from cold (donkeys are 'cold even in Tammuz'), while removing a saddle to cool the animal addresses no real suffering, so it remains forbidden.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to the Tosefta, why does an amulet not help an animal go out into the public domain even if it 'proved effective'?
A. Amulets never work on Shabbat
B. An amulet's effectiveness for a person relies on his guardian angel (mazal), which an animal lacks
C. Animals are always forbidden to wear amulets, proven or not
D. Only gold amulets are effective
Correct: B — The Gemara explains that an amulet's proven effectiveness applies specifically to a person, who has a mazal; an animal has no mazal, so the same amulet is not considered proven for it.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What is Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak's point about the decree prohibiting the treatment of a sick animal on Shabbat?
A. The decree applies universally with no dispute
B. The decree itself is subject to a tannaitic dispute, and the halakha follows the lenient opinion of Rabbi Oshaya
C. The decree only applies to donkeys
D. The decree was abolished entirely
Correct: B — He cites a baraita about an animal that ate vetch, where Rabbi Oshaya permits inducing movement to relieve dangerous constipation, and rules that the halakha follows Rabbi Oshaya's lenient view.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
In the story of the man who miraculously grew breasts to nurse his motherless son, what was Abaye's reaction to Rav Yosef's praise of the miracle?
A. He agreed completely
B. He said it was actually shameful, since the order of Creation had to be altered for this man
C. He denied the story happened
D. He said the miracle should have gone to the mother instead
Correct: B — Abaye responded 'on the contrary' — arguing that having Creation's order changed for oneself, even via a miracle, reflects poorly rather than well on the person.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak, what do 'levuvin' straps on rams actually accomplish?
A. They mark ownership
B. They are tied beneath the male organ to prevent the ram from mounting the ewes
C. They protect the ram's ears
D. They are purely decorative
Correct: B — Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak explains that the hide is tied beneath the ram's male organ specifically to prevent mounting, inferred from the parallel term 'sheḥuzot,' used for ewes to encourage mounting.
