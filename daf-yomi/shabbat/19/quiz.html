Shabbat 19 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Why is it permitted to leave food before a dog or a gentile in a courtyard, even if they carry it into the public domain?
A. Because carrying food is never forbidden on Shabbat
B. Because the Shabbat prohibition against carrying does not apply to a dog or a gentile
C. Because the courtyard owner is exempt from responsibility for animals
D. Because the food was placed there before Shabbat began
Correct: B — The baraita teaches that since neither the dog nor the gentile is obligated in the Shabbat prohibitions, one is not required to attend to or stop them from carrying the food out.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to Rabbi Yishmael, what may one do with garlic, unripe grapes, or wheat stalks crushed while it was still day on Friday?
A. Nothing further may be done with them at all
B. One may continue tending to them and finish extracting their liquid after dark
C. They must be discarded before Shabbat
D. One may only finish them if a gentile does the work
Correct: B — Rabbi Yishmael holds that since the additional liquid comes out 'on its own' from under the weight, one may keep tending to the process even after nightfall; Rabbi Akiva disagrees.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What is the practical difference between Rav's and Shmuel's positions on muktzeh in the case of a goat raised for its milk?
A. Rav permits slaughtering it for food on Shabbat, Shmuel forbids it
B. Rav forbids slaughtering it for food since it was designated for another purpose, Shmuel permits it
C. Both forbid slaughtering any animal on Shabbat
D. The dispute only concerns oil, not animals
Correct: B — Rav holds that an item designated for a non-food purpose (like a milk-goat) is muktzeh and may not be used for food on Shabbat; Shmuel holds muktzeh largely does not apply, so it is permitted.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why was a student who ruled leniently on muktzeh, in accordance with Shmuel's view, excommunicated by Rav Hamnuna?
A. Because his ruling was halakhically incorrect
B. Because he publicly ruled contrary to the practice of the local authority, Rav, in Rav's own town
C. Because he ruled on Shabbat itself, which is forbidden
D. Because he never studied the relevant sources
Correct: B — The Gemara affirms the halakha follows Rabbi Shimon (like Shmuel's leniency), but the student was punished for showing public disrespect by ruling against the accepted local practice in Rav's own territory.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to the new mishna, why may the Paschal lamb be lowered into the oven right at nightfall, unlike ordinary meat?
A. Because the Paschal lamb cooks especially fast
B. Because the members of the Paschal group are meticulous about mitzvot and won't come to stoke the coals
C. Because the Temple has no restrictions on Shabbat
D. Because the Paschal lamb is eaten raw
Correct: B — The Gemara explains the exception is due to the vigilance of the group members (bnei chavura zerizin hein), who can be trusted not to violate Shabbat by stoking the coals to speed the roasting.
