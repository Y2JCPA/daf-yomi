Shabbat 33 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to the Gemara's catalogue, what national catastrophe results from the sin of bloodshed?
A. Famine and drought
B. The Holy Temple is destroyed and the Divine Presence departs from Israel
C. Wild beasts multiply
D. Exile alone, without other consequences
Correct: B — The Gemara derives from Numbers 35:33–34 that bloodshed defiles the land to the point that God's presence can no longer dwell there, leading to the Temple's destruction.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
How does the Gemara resolve the objection that women, gentiles, and children — none obligated in Torah study — also suffer from askara if it is caused by dereliction in Torah study?
A. It admits Torah study is not the cause
B. It explains that each group causes idleness from Torah in others (wives distract husbands, gentiles distract Israel, children distract fathers)
C. It says askara has multiple unrelated causes
D. It claims the objection is baseless
Correct: B — The Gemara answers that although these groups are not themselves obligated in Torah study, they can cause others (husbands, Israel, fathers) to become idle from it, and are punished accordingly.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What happened to Rabbi Yehuda, Rabbi Yosei, and Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai after their conversation about Rome's public works was reported to the authorities?
A. All three were exiled equally
B. Rabbi Yehuda was elevated for praising Rome, Rabbi Yosei was exiled to Tzippori for his silence, and Rabbi Shimon was sentenced to death for his criticism
C. All three were executed
D. Nothing happened to any of them
Correct: B — Yehuda ben Gerim reported the conversation to the Roman authorities, resulting in different fates matched to each sage's stance: praise, silence, and denunciation.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
What sustained Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai and his son Rabbi Elazar during their twelve years hiding in a cave?
A. Food smuggled in by students
B. A miraculous carob tree and spring of water
C. They fasted the entire time
D. Supplies from Rabbi Shimon's wife
Correct: B — The Gemara relates that a miracle provided a carob tree and a spring of water, which sustained them throughout their twelve years in hiding.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What finally reconciled Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai to the world of ordinary people after he emerged from the cave a second time?
A. A letter from the Roman emperor
B. Seeing an elderly man running with two myrtle branches in honor of Shabbat, one for 'Remember' and one for 'Observe'
C. A vision from Elijah the Prophet
D. A ruling from the Sanhedrin
Correct: B — Witnessing the old man's simple, joyful devotion to the mitzva of honoring Shabbat with two myrtle branches showed Rabbi Shimon how beloved the mitzvot were to ordinary Israel, putting his mind at ease.
