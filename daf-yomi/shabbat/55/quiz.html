Shabbat 55 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Rabbi Aḥa bar Ḥanina, what is unique about God's decree in Ezekiel 9 to mark the righteous for protection?
A. It is the only decree ever given through an angel
B. It is the only good decree God ever retracted and turned to something bad
C. It only applied to the tribe of Levi
D. It was the first decree given after the Exodus
Correct: B — Rabbi Aḥa bar Ḥanina states that this is the only time a promise of good that emerged from God was later retracted and rendered evil, once Midat HaDin pointed out that the righteous too had failed to protest.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why does Midat HaDin argue that the righteous should not be fully protected in Ezekiel's vision?
A. They committed the same sins as the wicked
B. They had the ability to protest the wicked's conduct and did not do so
C. They were not actually righteous
D. They failed to bring sacrifices
Correct: B — Midat HaDin points out that it was in the power of the righteous to protest the sinful conduct around them, yet they did not, making them partially responsible.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
How does the Gemara ultimately refute Rav Ami's claim that there is no death or suffering without sin?
A. By citing a verse that explicitly contradicts him
B. By showing that a baraita listing four people who died only because of Adam's sin (not their own) follows a view compatible with death occurring without personal sin
C. By proving Rav Ami never made this statement
D. By citing a dissenting opinion of Rav Ami himself
Correct: B — The baraita about four people who died solely due to Adam's sin with the serpent, while personally sinless, directly contradicts Rav Ami's principle, leading the Gemara to declare it a conclusive refutation (teyuvta).
QUESTION 4 OF 5
What textual proof does the Gemara bring that Yishai, father of David, died only because of the serpent's sin?
A. A direct verse stating so explicitly
B. Abigail is called 'daughter of Nahash (the serpent)' even though she was actually Yishai's daughter
C. The Talmud simply states it as an oral tradition with no proof
D. A comparison to Moses' death
Correct: B — Since Abigail was demonstrably the daughter of Yishai (per Chronicles), her being called 'daughter of Nahash' is understood homiletically to mean she was the daughter of one who died because of the serpent's sin, i.e., Yishai.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to Rabban Gamliel's argument in defense of Reuben, why is it implausible that Reuben actually sinned with Bilhah?
A. Because the Torah never mentions Bilhah
B. Because Reuben's own descendants, the tribe of Reuben, would later stand at Mount Eival and curse anyone who lies with his father's wife
C. Because Bilhah died before the incident could occur
D. Because Jacob forbade any discussion of the matter
Correct: B — Rabban Gamliel reasons that it is inconceivable Reuben's own tribe would later curse 'he who lies with his father's wife' at Mount Eival if their ancestor had actually committed that sin.
