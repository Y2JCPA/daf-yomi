Berakhot 54 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to the mishna, which of the following is a 'vain prayer'?
A. Praying on entering a dangerous city
B. Praying that one's pregnant wife will give birth to a boy
C. Reciting a blessing over bad tidings
D. Thanking God for the past upon leaving a city
Correct: B — The mishna gives two examples of crying out over the past: praying about the sex of a child already conceived, and praying that a scream already heard in the city is not from one's own house.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
How does the mishna derive that one must bless for the bad as for the good?
A. From Jethro's blessing after the Exodus
B. From 'with all your might' — with every measure that He metes out to you, thank Him
C. From Boaz greeting the harvesters
D. From Psalm 107
Correct: B — The mishna expounds Deuteronomy 6:5: 'with all your heart' (both inclinations), 'with all your soul' (even if He takes your soul), 'with all your might' (all your money, or alternatively, every measure God metes out).
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What did the Gemara conclude from the stories of the man saved from a lion and of Mar son of Ravina?
A. Only miracles for the many warrant a blessing
B. For a miracle of the many everyone blesses; for a miracle of an individual, the individual himself blesses
C. An individual blesses only once, not every time he passes the place
D. Miracles for individuals require a thanksgiving offering
Correct: B — Rava told the man to bless whenever he reached the spot, and Mar son of Ravina blessed at both sites of his rescues; the Sages concluded that an individual is obligated to bless for his own miracle.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why does one recite 'the true Judge' at the site of Lot's wife, when the baraita speaks of thanks and praise?
A. Because Lot's wife was an idolater
B. Because Lot's wife is a tragedy; the baraita is emended so that one blesses 'the true Judge' for her and 'Who remembers the righteous' for Lot
C. Because the pillar of salt is a natural formation
D. Because no blessing is recited there at all
Correct: B — Since Lot's wife is a punishment, one says the True Judge; the baraita is read as requiring two blessings, the second recalling that even in His wrath God remembered Abraham and rescued Lot (Genesis 19:29).
QUESTION 5 OF 5
How was Rav Yehuda's obligation to give thanks after his illness fulfilled, given Abaye's requirement of ten and the fact that he did not recite the blessing himself?
A. The Sages exempted him because he was a scholar
B. He brought a thanksgiving offering instead
C. Ten people were present, and by answering amen to their blessing he was considered to have blessed himself
D. The obligation applies only to seafarers and desert travelers
Correct: C — Rav Ḥana of Baghdad and the Sages blessed God for Rav Yehuda's recovery in the presence of ten, and his amen counted as his own thanksgiving.
