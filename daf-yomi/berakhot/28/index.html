📖 Overview: A Day That Changed the Study Hall

Berakhot 28a–28b
כׇּל הֵיכָא דְּאָמְרִינַן ״בּוֹ בַּיּוֹם״ — הַהוּא יוֹמָא הֲוָה
The scene: The story of Rabban Gamliel's removal continues: Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya accepts the post, the doorkeeper is dismissed, hundreds of benches are added, and Eduyyot is taught that very day. Rabban Gamliel does not stay away — he debates Rabbi Yehoshua over an Ammonite convert, loses, and goes to make peace. The daf then returns to the mishna's timing of Mussaf: which prayer comes first when both are pending, what befalls one who prays late, and whether one may eat before Mussaf or Minḥa. A new mishna brings Rabbi Neḥunya ben Hakana's prayers on entering and leaving the study hall, followed by the final teachings of Rabbi Eliezer and Rabban Yoḥanan ben Zakkai. Finally the mishna on the Amida opens, and the Gemara asks why there are eighteen blessings — and why there are really nineteen.

👑 From Deposition to Shared Leadership

How Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya took office, and how Rabban Gamliel returned
1️⃣ His wife warns: 'Perhaps they will remove you too.' He answers with a proverb: use the precious goblet today even if it breaks tomorrow. 'You have no white hair' — he is eighteen, and eighteen rows of hair turn white. Hence: 'I am as one seventy years old'
↓
2️⃣ The doorkeeper is dismissed and all students may enter, reversing Rabban Gamliel's rule that only a student whose inside is like his outside (תּוֹכוֹ כְּבָרוֹ) may enter. Four hundred, or seven hundred, benches are added
↓
3️⃣ Rabban Gamliel is disheartened: 'Perhaps I withheld Torah from Israel.' He dreams of white jugs full of ashes — but the Gemara says this was shown only to ease his mind
↓
4️⃣ Eduyyot was taught that day; every 'on that day' (בּוֹ בַּיּוֹם) refers to it; no pending halakha went unresolved. Rabban Gamliel did not absent himself even for an hour
↓
5️⃣ Rabban Gamliel goes to appease Rabbi Yehoshua, sees his blackened walls: 'You are a blacksmith!' — 'Woe to a generation whose leader does not know how scholars make a living.' Forgiven only 'for my father's honor'
↓
6️⃣ A launderer carries word; Rabbi Yehoshua's message: 'the one who wears the uniform shall wear it.' Rabbi Akiva locks the gates. Rabbi Yehoshua comes himself: 'the sprinkler, son of a sprinkler, shall sprinkle'
↓
7️⃣ Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya cannot be demoted — one elevates in sanctity and does not lower (מַעֲלִין בַּקֹּדֶשׁ וְאֵין מוֹרִידִין). Rabban Gamliel lectures three weeks, Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya one. The student who started it all: Rabbi Shimon ben Yoḥai
Ḥagiga echo: This arrangement explains the phrase 'Whose week was it? It was Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya's week.'

🌍 May Yehuda the Ammonite Enter the Congregation?

Rabban Gamliel, though deposed, joins the halakhic debate — and loses
Yehuda
What is my status for entering the congregation of Israel?
R. Gamliel
You are forbidden. 'An Ammonite and a Moabite shall not enter the congregation of the Lord' (Deuteronomy 23:4).
R. Yehoshua
Do Ammon and Moab still sit in their place? Sennacherib came and scrambled all the nations — 'I have removed the bounds of the peoples' (Isaiah 10:13). And whatever separates, separates from the majority (כׇּל דְּפָרֵישׁ מֵרוּבָּא פָּרֵישׁ).
R. Gamliel
But it says 'afterward I will bring back the captivity of the children of Ammon' (Jeremiah 49:6) — and they have already returned!
R. Yehoshua
It also says 'I will turn the captivity of My people Israel' (Amos 9:14) — and they have not yet returned. Only proven facts count.
Result
They immediately permitted him to enter the congregation. Rabban Gamliel concluded: since the people follow Rabbi Yehoshua, I should go and appease him.
Method: Rabbi Yehoshua combines a historical fact (population transfer) with a legal presumption (follow the majority) — the individual convert is assumed to come from the permitted majority of nations.

🙏 Three Prayers of the Study Hall and the Deathbed

The mishna: Rabbi Neḥunya ben Hakana prayed briefly on entering and on leaving

On entering

  • 'May no mishap come about through me (שֶׁלֹּא יֶאֱרַע דְּבַר תַּקָּלָה עַל יָדִי)'
  • That I not fail in a matter of halakha, and my colleagues rejoice in me
  • That I not call the impure pure or the pure impure
  • That my colleagues not fail, and I rejoice in them

On leaving

  • 'I thank You that You placed my lot among those who sit in the study hall, not on street corners'
  • I rise early — for Torah; they rise early — for idle matters
  • I toil and receive reward; they toil and do not
  • I run to the World-to-Come; they run to the pit of destruction

Final teachings

  • Rabbi Eliezer: honor your colleagues, keep your children from higgayon, seat them at scholars' knees, and when you pray, know before Whom you stand
  • Rabban Yoḥanan ben Zakkai weeps: a mortal king's anger, prison, and death are temporary and he can be appeased — the King of Kings' are eternal, and two paths lie before me
  • His blessing: 'May the fear of Heaven be upon you like the fear of flesh and blood.' 'Only that much?' 'Would that it were! A sinner says: let no man see me'
  • At the end: clear the vessels from impurity, and set a chair for Hezekiah, King of Judah, who is coming
Common thread: Each prayer measures a person's life against the presence of God — in learning, in idleness, and at the threshold of judgment.

🔢 Eighteen Blessings — Corresponding to What?

The mishna: Rabban Gamliel says the full eighteen daily; Rabbi Yehoshua an abridged form; Rabbi Akiva — full only if fluent
❓ On what did the Sages base the number eighteen (שְׁמֹנֶה עֶשְׂרֵה)?
↓
R. Hillel b. R. Shmuel
The eighteen mentions of God's name in David's 'Give unto the Lord, O sons of might' (Psalm 29)
Rav Yosef
The eighteen mentions of God's name in the Shema
R. Tanḥum / RYbL
The eighteen vertebrae of the spine — so one bows until every vertebra protrudes
↓
⚠️ But are these eighteen? They are nineteen!
↓
💡 Rabbi Levi: the blessing of the heretics (בִּרְכַּת הַמִּינִים) was instituted later at Yavne. Its counterpart: 'the God of glory thunders' (Ps. 29:3); the word 'One' in Shema; the small vertebra at the base of the spine
↓
📜 Shimon HaPakuli arranged the eighteen in order before Rabban Gamliel at Yavne. 'Who can compose the blessing of the heretics?' Shmuel HaKatan stood and composed it — and the next year he forgot it
How low to bow: Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi: until the vertebrae protrude. Ulla: until one could see a small coin (issar) opposite his heart. Rabbi Ḥanina: once the head is moved forward it is enough. Rava: only if he strains and appears to be bowing.
