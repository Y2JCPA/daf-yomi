Berakhot 28 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
How did Rabbi Yehoshua answer Rabban Gamliel's proof from 'an Ammonite and a Moabite shall not enter the congregation'?
A. The verse applies only to Moabites, not Ammonites
B. Sennacherib scrambled the nations, so today's Ammonites are not ethnic Ammonites, and we follow the majority
C. Converts are never included in the prohibition
D. The prohibition lapsed after the destruction of the Temple
Correct: B — Rabbi Yehoshua cited Isaiah 10:13 on Sennacherib's population transfers and the principle that whatever separates is presumed to come from the majority. When Rabban Gamliel cited Ammon's return (Jeremiah 49:6), he replied that Israel's return (Amos 9:14) has also not yet happened.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why was Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya not simply removed when Rabban Gamliel was restored?
A. Because he had been appointed for life
B. Because the Sages feared his wealth
C. Because one elevates in sanctity and does not lower
D. Because Rabban Gamliel refused to return without him
Correct: C — The tradition 'one elevates in sanctity and does not lower' prevented demoting him. Alternating weekly would breed jealousy, so Rabban Gamliel lectured three weeks and Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya one.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
If both Minḥa and Mussaf are pending, which is recited first according to the halakha as ruled by Rabbi Yoḥanan?
A. Mussaf, because its time is about to elapse
B. Minḥa, because it is the more frequent prayer
C. Whichever the person prefers
D. Mussaf, because it corresponds to a sacrifice
Correct: B — The Rabbis hold that the frequent practice takes precedence, so Minḥa comes first; Rabbi Yehuda prefers Mussaf since its time elapses. Rabbi Yoḥanan ruled like the Rabbis, a ruling Rabbi Natan bar Tovi learned from him forty times.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
When Rabban Yoḥanan ben Zakkai blessed his students that their fear of Heaven be like their fear of flesh and blood, they asked 'only that much?' What did he answer?
A. That fearing God more than man is impossible for humans
B. Would that you reached that level — a sinner says only 'let no man see me'
C. That fear of man is greater than fear of God
D. That he was too weak to bless them further
Correct: B — He explained that people sin when they think no human is watching. If one feared God as much as he fears being seen by others, he would never sin — so the blessing is actually a high standard.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
The Gemara notes that the 'eighteen' blessings are really nineteen. How does Rabbi Levi resolve this?
A. Two of the blessings were originally one
B. The blessing of the heretics was instituted later at Yavne
C. The nineteenth is only recited on Shabbat
D. Psalm 29 actually mentions God's name nineteen times
Correct: B — Rabbi Levi explains that the blessing of the heretics was added at Yavne, where Shmuel HaKatan composed it at Rabban Gamliel's request. Each of the three correspondences is then extended to cover the nineteenth blessing.
