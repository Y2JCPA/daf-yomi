📖 Overview: Blessings Over Food Begin

Berakhot 35a–35b
אָסוּר לוֹ לָאָדָם שֶׁיֵּהָנֶה מִן הָעוֹלָם הַזֶּה בְּלֹא בְּרָכָה
The scene: A new chapter opens with the mishna that assigns a blessing to every category of food: borei peri ha'etz on tree fruit, borei peri hagafen on wine, borei peri ha'adama on produce of the ground, hamotzi on bread. The Gemara immediately asks where the Torah obligates a blessing before eating at all, and after chasing the verse 'sanctified for praises' through a maze of derivations, concludes that the obligation rests on reason: the world belongs to God, and enjoying it without a blessing is like misusing consecrated property. That thought spills into the famous debate over how a Jew should balance Torah study with earning a living. Finally the Gemara returns to the mishna and asks why wine, unlike olive oil, earned a blessing of its own.

🍇 The Mishna: One Blessing for Each Category

Two 'exceptions' are really upgrades for especially significant foods
FoodBlessingNote
Fruit of the treeבּוֹרֵא פְּרִי הָעֵץ — Who creates fruit of the treeDefault for anything growing on a tree
Wineבּוֹרֵא פְּרִי הַגֶּפֶן — Who creates fruit of the vineException: significant enough to get its own formula
Fruit of the groundבּוֹרֵא פְּרִי הָאֲדָמָה — Who creates fruit of the groundDefault for produce that does not grow on a tree
Breadהַמּוֹצִיא לֶחֶם מִן הָאָרֶץ — Who brings forth bread from the earthException: bread's importance earns a distinct blessing
Herbs and leafy vegetablesבּוֹרֵא פְּרִי הָאֲדָמָהRabbi Yehuda: בּוֹרֵא מִינֵי דְשָׁאִים, 'Who creates various kinds of herbs'
Rabbi Yehuda's view: He sees a difference in quality between ordinary produce of the ground and leafy herbs, so he assigns herbs a formula of their own. The Rabbis lump them together under 'fruit of the ground'.

📜 Where Does the Torah Require a Blessing Before Eating?

The Gemara tries one derivation after another, and each one leaks
❓ 'Sanctified for praises (הִלּוּלִים) before the Lord' (Lev. 19:24) — the plural teaches a blessing before and after. Rabbi Akiva: one may not taste anything before blessing.
↓
⚠️ But that verse is already used: one hillul = ḥillul, 'redeem, then eat'; the other = only what needs song (wine libation, Judges 9:13) needs redemption
↓
Teaches 'fourth-year vineyard'
If he learns 'vineyard' from Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi's gezera shava (תְּבוּאָתוֹ / תְּבוּאַת הַכָּרֶם), one hillul is left over for the blessing — but that gives only the blessing after; 'before' comes from a kal vaḥomer (if satiated he blesses, when hungry all the more so)
Teaches 'fourth-year sapling'
Works for anything planted — but meat, eggs and fish are not planted. Where is their blessing?
↓
🔁 Extending from vineyard to all foods also fails: vineyard is special (olelot), grain is special (ḥalla), their common denominator is 'offered on the altar', the seven species are special (bikkurim)
↓
✅ Rather, it is sevara — reason itself: אָסוּר לוֹ לָאָדָם שֶׁיֵּהָנֶה מִן הָעוֹלָם הַזֶּה בְּלֹא בְּרָכָה, one may not benefit from this world without a blessing
Side note: Rav Pappa closes a loophole in the derivation: an olive grove is called kerem zayit (Judges 15:5), never plain kerem, so the olive cannot ride on the vineyard's status.

🌍 Eating Without a Blessing: Three Descriptions of the Offense

The Amoraim reach for the language of sacrilege and theft

Me'ila — Misuse of Sanctity

  • Tosefta: whoever benefits without a blessing is as if he committed me'ila
  • Rav Yehuda in Shmuel's name: as if he enjoyed consecrated property of Heaven — 'The earth and all it contains is the Lord's' (Ps. 24:1)
  • Remedy? 'Go to a Sage.' Rava: go beforehand and let him teach you blessings, so you never come to me'ila

Rabbi Levi's Resolution

  • Contradiction: 'the earth is the Lord's' (Ps. 24:1) vs. 'the earth He gave to mankind' (Ps. 115:16)
  • Before a blessing — the earth is the Lord's
  • After a blessing — He has given it to mankind

Theft from Father and Mother

  • Rabbi Ḥanina bar Pappa: as if he robs God and the community of Israel — 'Whoever robs his father and mother… is companion to a destroyer' (Prov. 28:24)
  • 'Father' = God ('Is He not your Father Who created you', Deut. 32:6); 'mother' = Knesset Yisrael ('do not forsake the Torah of your mother', Prov. 1:8)
  • 'Companion to a destroyer' = companion of Jeroboam ben Nevat, who corrupted Israel
Grain, too: Rabbi Ḥanina bar Pappa also reconciles 'I will take back My grain' (Hosea 2:11) with 'you shall gather your grain' (Deut. 11:14): the grain is theirs when Israel does God's will, His when they do not.

🌾 'You Shall Gather Your Grain' — Torah Study vs. Earning a Living

A tannaitic dispute, then the Amoraim weigh in with hard experience
R' Yishmael
'This Torah shall not depart from your mouth' (Josh. 1:8) could be read literally. So the Torah adds 'you shall gather your grain' — conduct yourself in the way of the world: Torah and work.
R' Shimon b.Y.
Plow in plowing season, sow, harvest, thresh, winnow — what will become of Torah? When Israel does God's will, 'strangers will stand and feed your flocks' (Isa. 61:5). 'Gather your grain' describes a time when they do not.
Abaye
Many did as Rabbi Yishmael and it succeeded for them; many did as Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai and it did not succeed for them.
Rava
To his students: I beg you, in Nisan and Tishrei do not appear before me, so that you are not preoccupied with your livelihood the whole year.
R' Yoḥanan
In the name of Rabbi Yehuda son of Rabbi El'ai: the earlier generations made Torah fixed and work occasional, and both endured. The later generations made work fixed and Torah occasional, and neither endured.
R' Yoḥanan
Likewise, earlier generations brought produce in through the front gate to obligate it in tithes; later ones bring it through roofs and courtyards to exempt it. (Rabbi Yannai: tithes take hold only when produce 'sees the front of the house'; Rabbi Yoḥanan himself: even a courtyard fixes it.)
Connection: The verse 'you shall gather your grain' entered the discussion to explain who owns the grain; the Gemara follows it into the question of how much of life belongs to the field.

🍷 Why Does Wine Get 'Fruit of the Vine' While Olive Oil Does Not?

Every proposed distinction between wine and oil is tested against a source
1️⃣ Proposal: wine's fruit was changed for the better, so its blessing changed. Objection: olive oil also changed for the better, yet Shmuel and Rabbi Yoḥanan rule its blessing stays borei peri ha'etz
↓
2️⃣ Answer: no fitting formula exists for oil — 'fruit of the olive' names the olive itself. Objection: say 'fruit of the olive tree'!
↓
3️⃣ Mar Zutra: wine nourishes (זָיֵין), oil does not. Objection: one who vows off 'nourishment' is permitted only water and salt, so everything else, oil included, nourishes (Rav Huna reads that vow as 'anything that nourishes')
↓
4️⃣ Rather: wine satisfies (סָעֵיד), oil does not. Objection: Rava drank wine all Passover eve to whet his appetite for matza! Answer: a lot stimulates, a little satisfies
↓
5️⃣ Objection: 'wine gladdens the heart of man… and bread satisfies' (Ps. 104:15) — bread satisfies, not wine! Answer: wine has two virtues, it satisfies and it gladdens; bread satisfies but does not gladden
↓
6️⃣ Then recite the three blessings of Grace after wine? People do not base a meal on it. Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak: and if one does? Rava: when Elijah comes he will tell us; for now his intention is nullified by everyone else's
Olive oil itself: When is borei peri ha'etz said over oil? Not when drunk plain — that harms (one who drinks teruma oil pays the principal but not the fifth), and not when dipped with bread, where the bread is primary and exempts the oil. The Gemara turns to oil taken in anigeron, beet water, and the discussion continues on the next daf.

📋 Berakhot 35 — Summary Map

📖 Mishna: tree fruit → ha'etz; wine → hagafen; ground → ha'adama; bread → hamotzi; vegetables → ha'adama (R' Yehuda: minei deshaim)
↓
❓ Source for a blessing before eating? 'Kodesh hillulim', gezera shava, kal vaḥomer, common denominators, seven species — each fails (meat, eggs, fish!)
↓
💡 Sevara: forbidden to benefit from this world without a blessing; doing so is me'ila, theft from Father and Mother. Rava: learn blessings from a Sage in advance
↓
🌍 Ps. 24:1 before a blessing; Ps. 115:16 after. 'Gather your grain' → R' Yishmael vs. R' Shimon b. Yoḥai; Abaye: R' Yishmael's way succeeded
🍷 Wine vs. oil: wine both satisfies and gladdens, so it earns hagafen; still no Grace after Meals, since people do not base meals on it
↓
🎓 Key Principle: A blessing is the act that turns God's property into ours — before it, 'the earth is the Lord's'; after it, 'the earth He gave to mankind'
