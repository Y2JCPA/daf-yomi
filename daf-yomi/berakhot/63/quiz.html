QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Rava, why is a shortcut through a synagogue forbidden while wearing shoes and spitting are permitted?
A. Because the synagogue has the sanctity of the Temple Mount
B. Because a synagogue is like one's house: people object to a shortcut through their home but not to shoes or spitting there
C. Because shoes and spitting are only forbidden during prayer
D. Because a shortcut wears down the synagogue floor
Correct: B — Rava's principle is 'like one's house' (כִּי בֵיתוֹ): whatever a person would resent in his own home is forbidden in a synagogue, and whatever he tolerates there is permitted.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
How does Rava read the verse 'It is time to act for the Lord; they have made void Your Torah' (Psalms 119:126)?
A. Only from beginning to end: because Torah was voided, it is time to act
B. Only from end to beginning: Torah may be voided because it is time to act
C. In both directions: each half can be the reason for the other
D. As a prohibition against ever setting aside any part of Torah
Correct: C — Rava says the verse is expounded from beginning to end and from end to beginning — voiding Torah demands action for the Lord, and at times acting for the Lord justifies setting Torah aside.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What does Abaye infer from bar Kappara's 'where there is no man, there be a man', and when is the inference needed?
A. That one should always seek positions of leadership; it is needed for the humble
B. That where there is a man, do not be a man; it is needed even where both are equally qualified
C. That only a scholar may serve; it is needed for unlearned communities
D. That leadership passes by inheritance; it is needed when a leader dies
Correct: B — Abaye infers that where someone already fills the role, one should not push in. The Gemara says this is only needed where the two are equal — otherwise it would be obvious.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why did the Sages of Eretz Yisrael go to such lengths to stop Ḥanina from intercalating years in Babylonia?
A. Because Ḥanina had never received ordination
B. Because his calculations were mathematically wrong
C. Because 'out of Zion shall go forth the Torah, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem' (Isaiah 2:3)
D. Because Rabbi Akiva had forbidden it explicitly
Correct: C — The Gemara asks 'why all this?' and answers with Isaiah 2:3: Torah authority, including fixing the calendar, must emanate from Zion. Ḥanina's appeal to Rabbi Akiva's precedent was rejected because Akiva left no equal behind, while Ḥanina's 'kids had become goats with horns'.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
In Rabbi Yehuda's exposition at Yavne, what is the first meaning given to the word הַסְכֵּת in 'Keep silence and hear, Israel'?
A. Form groups (עֲשׂוּ כִּתּוֹת) and study Torah, since Torah is acquired only in company
B. Fast and afflict yourselves before studying Torah
C. Write down every teaching you hear
D. Study only from a single teacher
Correct: A — The Gemara reads hasket as asu kitot — form groups — citing Rabbi Yosei son of Rabbi Ḥanina that a sword is upon those who study alone, who grow foolish and sin. Later readings add 'break yourselves over Torah' (Reish Lakish) and 'be silent, then analyze' (Rava).
