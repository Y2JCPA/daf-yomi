Berakhot 5 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Abaye, what must even a Torah scholar do before sleeping?
A. Recite the entire Shema with its blessings
B. Recite at least one verse of mercy, such as 'Into Your hand I entrust my spirit'
C. Study one chapter of Mishna
D. Recite Psalm 4 in full
Correct: B — The Gemara had said a Torah scholar need not recite Shema on his bed, but Abaye requires him to say at least one verse of prayer for mercy, such as Psalms 31:6.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
In Reish Lakish's four-step strategy against the evil inclination, what is the final resort?
A. Fasting
B. Reciting Shema
C. Reminding oneself of the day of death
D. Engaging in Torah study
Correct: C — The order is: incite the good inclination; if that fails, study Torah; if that fails, recite Shema; if that fails, remember the day of death — derived from 'and be still, Selah'.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to Rava, if a person suffering examines his deeds and finds no transgression, what should he do?
A. Conclude that the suffering is a mistake
B. Attribute it to neglect of Torah study
C. Fast until the suffering passes
D. Ask the Sages to pray on his behalf
Correct: B — Having found no sin, he attributes the suffering to bitul Torah, as in 'Happy is the man whom You afflict, Lord, and teach from Your Torah'. Only if that too does not apply are they afflictions of love.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why, according to the Gemara, could Rabbi Yoḥanan not heal himself as he healed his students?
A. His own suffering was an affliction of love and had to be endured
B. A prisoner cannot free himself from prison
C. He had not accepted his suffering knowingly
D. Only Rabbi Ḥanina had the power to heal
Correct: B — The Gemara asks why Rabbi Yoḥanan waited for Rabbi Ḥanina and answers with the proverb that a prisoner cannot release himself from prison.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What flaw did the Sages identify when Rav Huna's four hundred barrels of wine turned to vinegar?
A. He had delayed the afternoon prayer
B. He had not given his tenant farmer his share of the vines
C. He sold wine at an unfair price
D. He had not placed his bed north to south
Correct: B — Rav Huna defended himself that the tenant stole from him, but the Sages answered that one who steals from a thief still tastes theft. When he undertook to give the share, his loss was restored.
