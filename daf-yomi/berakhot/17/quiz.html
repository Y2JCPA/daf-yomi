Berakhot 17 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Which sage's prayer after the Amida — 'before I was created I was worthless… I am dust in life, all the more so in my death' — is identified as Rav Hamnuna Zuti's confession on Yom Kippur?
A. Rabbi Alexandri
B. Rava
C. Mar son of Ravina
D. Rav Sheshet
Correct: B — Rava's prayer asks that his sins be cleansed in God's mercy but not through suffering and serious illness; the Gemara notes this is the confession Rav Hamnuna Zuti recited on Yom Kippur.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What did Rav Sheshet ask of God after praying on a fast day?
A. That his fast atone for the whole world
B. That his diminished fat and blood be considered as if offered on the altar
C. That he be granted long life and sustenance
D. That the Temple be rebuilt speedily
Correct: B — Rav Sheshet noted that in Temple times only a sacrifice's fat and blood were offered, yet the sinner was atoned; he asked that the fat and blood he lost by fasting be regarded as if he had offered them on the altar.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to the saying of the Sages of Yavne, why should a Torah scholar not feel superior to a laborer who studies little?
A. Because the laborer supports the scholar financially
B. Because one who does much and one who does little are equal, provided one directs his heart to Heaven
C. Because Torah study is only obligatory for scholars
D. Because the laborer's work is itself a form of Torah study
Correct: B — The Sages of Yavne said: I am a creature and my fellow is a creature; just as he does not presume to do my work, I do not presume to do his. And lest you say 'I do much and he does little', we learned that both are equal as long as one directs his heart to Heaven.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
How does Rav Sheisha son of Rav Idi explain why the Rabbis allow a groom to recite Shema but consider a scholar's idleness on the Ninth of Av presumptuous?
A. The attribution of the opinions must be reversed
B. Everyone recites Shema, so the groom is not conspicuous; everyone works on the Ninth of Av, so the idle man is conspicuous
C. Shema is a Torah obligation while the Ninth of Av is rabbinic
D. A groom cannot concentrate, so his recitation is not really presumptuous
Correct: B — Rabbi Yoḥanan resolved the contradiction by reversing the attributions, but Rav Sheisha kept them: with Shema, since everyone recites, the groom does not stand out; on the Ninth of Av, since everyone works, one who is idle looks presumptuous.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to the new mishna (Mi SheMeto), which pallbearers are exempt from reciting Shema?
A. All pallbearers and their replacements, in every position
B. Only those who have already carried the bier
C. Those before the bier who are still needed to carry it; those after the bier are obligated
D. None — pallbearers are exempt only from the Amida
Correct: C — The mishna distinguishes: those positioned before the bier who are needed to carry it are exempt from Shema, while those after the bier, having already carried, are obligated. Both groups, however, are exempt from the Amida prayer.
