Berakhot 45 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Which of the following diners counts toward the three needed for zimmun according to the mishna?
A. A waiter who ate less than an olive-bulk
B. One who ate untithed produce (tevel)
C. A Samaritan (Kuti) who ate with two Jews
D. A gentile who ate with two Jews
Correct: C — The mishna lists the Kuti among those who count, alongside a waiter who ate an olive-bulk and eaters of demai and redeemed tithes. A gentile, a tevel-eater, and a waiter who ate less than an olive-bulk do not count.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What does Rav Ḥanan bar Abba derive from 'Praise God with me, and we will exalt His name together'?
A. That zimmun requires ten people
B. That one answering amen should not raise his voice louder than the one blessing
C. That women may not form a zimmun
D. That the translator must speak louder than the reader
Correct: B — The word 'together' teaches that the response should match, not exceed, the leader's voice. Rabbi Shimon ben Pazi derives the parallel rule for the translator from 'God responded in a voice' — in Moses' voice.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
How does the Gemara answer the proof from the baraita that women form a zimmun for themselves, even though a hundred women are like two men for a quorum?
A. Women are exempt from zimmun entirely
B. There are three separate minds among three women, which two men lack
C. The baraita follows Rav, not Rabbi Yoḥanan
D. Women may only form a zimmun with slaves
Correct: B — The Gemara says 'there it is different because there are minds' (דֵּעוֹת) — three individuals can fulfil 'praise God with me… together', while two men cannot. Women and slaves may not combine only because of concern for promiscuity.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why is the halakha in accordance with Mar Zutra that for a zimmun of ten the absent diner must actually return?
A. Because nine do not look like ten
B. Because a zimmun of ten invokes the Name of Heaven, and it is not proper conduct to do so with fewer than ten present
C. Because Rav Ashi agreed with him
D. Because the absent diner cannot hear from the market
Correct: B — Rav Ashi argued the opposite was more logical (nine look like ten, two do not look like three), but the Gemara rules like Mar Zutra because mentioning God's Name with fewer than ten present is not proper.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
One arrives and finds the group saying 'Nevarekh'. What does he answer, and what did Mareimar tell the three Sages who each blessed alone?
A. Amen; they should form a zimmun now
B. 'Barukh u'mevorakh'; they fulfilled Grace but not zimmun, and there is no retroactive zimmun
C. 'Barukh u'mevorakh'; they must recite Grace again
D. Nothing; two of them may still form a zimmun
Correct: B — Rav Zevid: at 'Nevarekh' one answers 'Blessed is He and blessed is His Name'; at 'Barukh' one answers amen (Rav Pappa). Mareimar told the Sages that their obligation of Grace was fulfilled but zimmun was lost — אֵין זִימּוּן לְמַפְרֵעַ.
