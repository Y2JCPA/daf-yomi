Berakhot 61 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
How does Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak refute the derasha that the double yod of vayyitzer teaches two inclinations?
A. Because the verse is speaking about the angels, not about man
B. Because animals, whose creation is not written with a double yod, clearly do have an inclination — they damage, bite and kick
C. Because Adam had no inclination before eating from the tree
D. Because the word is also spelled with one yod elsewhere
Correct: B — The objection is from animals: if the double yod were what conferred an inclination, animals would have none, yet they plainly do.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to the opinion that the tzela was a whole face, what does 'And the Lord God built the tzela' mean?
A. That God constructed Eve from scratch out of a rib
B. That God braided Eve's hair before bringing her to Adam, since braiding is called 'building' in the coastal towns
C. That God built the Garden of Eden around her
D. That God built a chamber for Adam and Eve to live in
Correct: B — Since on this view nothing new needed building, the verse is read homiletically per Rabbi Shimon ben Menasya. Rav Ḥisda alternatively read it as her physical form, built like a storehouse — narrow above, wide below.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
How did Rabba describe himself, and how did Abaye respond?
A. Rabba said he was righteous, and Abaye agreed
B. Rabba said he was middling, and Abaye protested that he leaves no room for any creature to live
C. Rabba said he was wicked, and Abaye consoled him
D. Rabba refused to categorize himself, and Abaye praised his humility
Correct: B — Rabba said 'people like us are middling', and Abaye objected that if a man like Rabba is only middling, no one else could hope to be more.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
What is the point of Rabbi Akiva's parable of the fox and the fish?
A. That one should flee persecution rather than confront it
B. That clever advice from an enemy should still be considered
C. That if Jews are endangered while studying Torah — their element of life — abandoning it would be far more dangerous
D. That fish are wiser than land animals
Correct: C — The fish refuse to leave the water that gives them life. So too, said Rabbi Akiva, Torah is 'your life and the length of your days'; leaving it is the truly fatal habitat.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What did Rabbi Akiva tell his students as he was being executed while reciting Shema?
A. That he was suffering for the sins of his generation
B. That he had been troubled all his days by 'with all your soul' and would not miss the chance to fulfil it
C. That the pain was not as great as it appeared
D. That he expected the Romans to relent
Correct: B — He explained that 'with all your soul' means even if God takes your soul, and he prolonged the word 'One' until his soul departed on it.
