Berakhot 56 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
How did Rabbi Yehoshua son of Rabbi Ḥananya make the emperor see a specific dream?
A. He prayed for it during the Priestly Blessing
B. He described the scene to him, and the emperor thought about it all day and saw it that night
C. He had it interpreted before three people
D. He wrote the dream in a book the emperor read
Correct: B — Rabbi Yehoshua told the emperor he would see the Persians enslave him; the emperor thought about it the whole day and dreamed it that night. Shmuel did the same with King Shapur.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What determined whether bar Haddaya interpreted a dream favorably or unfavorably?
A. Whether the dreamer was a Torah scholar
B. Whether the dream occurred in the morning
C. Whether the dreamer paid him a fee
D. Whether the dream quoted a verse from Deuteronomy
Correct: C — Bar Haddaya interpreted favorably for one who paid and unfavorably for one who did not; Abaye paid and Rava did not, so the same dreams received opposite readings.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What did Rava find written in bar Haddaya's book, and why did it enrage him?
A. A list of his debts, which he had never been told about
B. 'All dreams follow the mouth' — proving the calamities had depended on the interpreter's choice
C. The name of the thief who robbed the king's treasury
D. A curse written against Rava's household
Correct: B — The book said 'all dreams follow the mouth'; Rava realized his suffering had been in bar Haddaya's hands, forgave everything except the prediction about his wife, and cursed him.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
According to Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi, what should one do after dreaming of a river, bird, or pot?
A. Fast the next day
B. Rise early and recite the verse with the favorable meaning before the negative verse can precede it
C. Tell the dream only to a paid interpreter
D. Recite the blessing 'Who makes creatures different'
Correct: B — Since these images appear in Scripture in both good and bad contexts, one should rise early and recite the good verse, such as 'I will extend peace to her like a river', before the negative verse can take effect.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
The baraita lists five things about an ox in a dream. How does the Gemara reconcile 'riding it — he rises to greatness' with another baraita, 'riding it — he will die'?
A. One refers to a white ox and the other to a red ox
B. One refers to a scholar and the other to an ignoramus
C. One refers to a man riding the ox, the other to the ox riding the man
D. One refers to a dream in the morning and the other at night
Correct: C — The Gemara answers 'not difficult': when he rides the ox he rises to greatness; when the ox rides him it is a sign of death.
