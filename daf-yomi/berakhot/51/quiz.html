Berakhot 51 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
What did Rav Ḥisda mean by asking whether one who ate garlic should eat another garlic?
A. That one who forgot a blessing should skip the rest of the meal
B. That one who forgot to bless must bless before continuing to eat, rather than adding to the lapse
C. That garlic does not require any blessing
D. That blessings are never recited after eating has begun
Correct: B — Rav Ḥisda's analogy teaches that one should not compound the failure to bless by continuing to eat without a blessing; he must recite it before going on.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why does the Gemara reject Ravina's proof from the blessing over immersion?
A. Because immersion has no blessing at all
B. Because the immersion blessing is recited before immersing
C. Because before immersing the person was unfit to bless, whereas before eating he was fit and is now excluded
D. Because eating is a rabbinic obligation and immersion a Torah obligation
Correct: C — In the case of immersion the man could not bless earlier because he was impure; one who could have blessed before eating and did not has been excluded, and 'since he was excluded, he is excluded'.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
How does the Gemara resolve the baraitot that say asparagus is both good and harmful for the intestines, and that spitting after it both does and does not cause harm?
A. One baraita refers to asparagus made of wine, the other to asparagus made of beer
B. One refers to morning, the other to evening
C. One refers to the sick, the other to the healthy
D. The baraitot are simply in dispute
Correct: A — Every contradiction on the daf about asparagus is resolved the same way: one baraita speaks of wine-based asparagus and the other of beer-based.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
According to Rabbi Yoḥanan, which of the ten things said about the cup of blessing do 'we' actually have?
A. Adorning, wrapping, lifting, and gazing
B. Rinsing, washing, undiluted wine, and a full cup
C. Both hands, right hand, a handbreadth, and a gift to the household
D. All ten
Correct: B — Rabbi Yoḥanan said: we have only four — rinsing (inside), washing (outside), undiluted, and full.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
Why did Ulla refuse to send the cup of blessing to Yalta?
A. Because women may not drink from the cup of blessing
B. Because the cup had already been drunk
C. Because Rabbi Yoḥanan taught that a woman's offspring are blessed through the man, from 'He will bless the fruit of your body'
D. Because the cup was a second cup, a cup of punishment
Correct: C — Ulla cited Rabbi Yoḥanan: the verse says 'the fruit of your [masculine] body', so giving the cup to Rav Naḥman suffices for his wife to be blessed. Yalta responded by breaking four hundred barrels of wine.
