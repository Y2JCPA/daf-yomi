📖 Overview: Short Prayers and Missed Words

Berakhot 29a–29b
הֲבִינֵנוּ ה׳ אֱלֹהֵינוּ לָדַעַת דְּרָכֶיךָ… טֶרֶם נִקְרָא אַתָּה תַעֲנֶה
The scene: Shmuel HaKatan, who composed the blessing against heretics, forgot it the next year and was left standing for hours without being removed — the Gemara asks why, and along the way debates whether a righteous person can become wicked, using Yoḥanan the High Priest as the test case. After counting the blessings of the Shabbat, Rosh HaShana, and fast-day Amida, the daf turns to Rabbi Yehoshua's abridged Amida: Shmuel's 'Havineinu' text, and why it cannot be used on motzaei Shabbat or in the rainy season. That leads to Rabbi Tanḥum's rules for one who forgot rain, havdala, or Rosh Ḥodesh. The daf closes with four definitions of a 'fixed' prayer, the meaning of 'parashat ha'ibur', the brief prayers for a place of danger, and the traveler's prayer.

🕯️ Why Wasn't Shmuel HaKatan Removed?

He forgot the blessing of the heretics, scrutinized it for two or three hours, and stayed as prayer leader
Gemara
Rav Yehuda said in Rav's name: one who errs in any blessing is not removed, but in the blessing of the heretics he is removed — we suspect he is a heretic. So why not Shmuel HaKatan?
Answer
Shmuel HaKatan is different: he instituted the blessing himself.
Gemara
Perhaps he reconsidered and changed?
Abaye
We have a tradition: a good person does not become wicked (טָבָא לָא הָוֵי בִּישָׁא). Ezekiel 18:24, 'when the righteous returns from his righteousness', speaks of one wicked from the outset who repented and relapsed.
Gemara
But the mishna says 'do not trust yourself until the day you die' — Yoḥanan the High Priest served eighty years and became a Sadducee!
Abaye / Rava
Abaye: Yannai and Yoḥanan are the same man, wicked from the outset. Rava: they are two — Yannai wicked from the start, Yoḥanan righteous from the start. So for Rava, even the initially righteous may change.
Final answer
Shmuel HaKatan is different because he had begun the blessing. Rav (or Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi): removal applies only where he never began it; if he began, he may finish.
Corresponding to what? Seven Shabbat blessings — the seven 'voices' of David over the waters (Psalm 29), per Rabbi Ḥalafta ben Shaul. Nine of Rosh HaShana — Hannah's nine mentions of God's name, for on Rosh HaShana Sarah, Rachel, and Hannah were remembered (Rabbi Yitzḥak of Kartignin). Twenty-four of fast days — Solomon's twenty-four songs when the ark entered the Holy of Holies, said on a day of mercy (Rabbi Ḥelbo).

✂️ Havineinu: The Abridged Amida and Its Limits

What is Rabbi Yehoshua's 'abridgement of the eighteen'?
❓ Rav: an abridged form of each blessing. Shmuel: one blessing, 'Havineinu' (הֲבִינֵנוּ), replacing the thirteen middle blessings and ending 'Who listens to prayer'
↓
⚠️ Abaye would curse anyone who prayed Havineinu — it is only for exigent circumstances
↓
Not on motzaei Shabbat / Festivals
Rav Naḥman in Shmuel's name: havdala must be said in 'Who graciously grants knowledge'
Not in the rainy season
Rav Beivai bar Abaye: the request for rain must be said in 'the blessing of the years'
↓
🗣️ Rabba bar Shmuel: say havdala as a fourth blessing, like Rabbi Akiva? — No: they instituted seven, not eight, just as we do not follow Rabbi Akiva all year (eighteen, not nineteen)
↓
🗣️ Mar Zutra: fold it in — 'Havineinu… Who distinguishes sacred from profane'? For havdala: kashya, unanswered. For rain, 'satisfy us… and grant dew and rain'? He will become confused — a change in the middle confuses, at the beginning it does not
↓
🗣️ Rav Ashi: put rain in 'Who listens to prayer', as one who forgot may? — One who erred is different; not ab initio
Havineinu's requests: Understanding, repentance, forgiveness, redemption, healing, prosperity, ingathering, judgment, the wicked, the righteous, Jerusalem, David's line — 'before we call, You answer'.

🌧️ Forgot Rain, Havdala, or Rosh Ḥodesh — Go Back?

Rabbi Tanḥum in the name of Rav Asi, and the Tosefta that seems to contradict him

Must return to the beginning

  • Omitted 'the might of the rains' in 'Who revives the dead' — always
  • Omitted the request for rain in 'the blessing of the years' and remembered only after 'Who listens to prayer' (the Tosefta's case)
  • Omitted Rosh Ḥodesh in the Temple-service blessing and already finished and moved his feet (Rav Pappa son of Rav Aḥa bar Adda, from Rav)

Need not return to the beginning

  • Omitted the request for rain but remembered before 'Who listens to prayer' — he asks there
  • Omitted havdala in 'Who graciously grants knowledge' — he can say it over the cup of wine
  • Omitted Rosh Ḥodesh and remembered in thanksgiving or 'Grant peace' — he returns only to the Temple-service blessing
  • Finished but still standing, or accustomed to supplications after the Amida — he returns to Temple service (Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak; a second version reverses the last condition)
Rejected harmonization: The Gemara first suggested the strict Tosefta speaks of an individual and the lenient ruling of a congregation that hears the prayer leader. But then Rabbi Tanḥum should have said 'because he hears it from the leader', not 'because he can say it in Who listens to prayer'. So both speak of an individual, and the difference is before or after that blessing.

📿 'One Whose Prayer Is Fixed' — Four Definitions

The mishna: Rabbi Eliezer says one who makes his prayer keva (קֶבַע), his prayer is not supplication
WhoWhat 'fixed' meansComment
Rabbi Ya'akov bar Idi in the name of Rabbi OshayaAnyone for whom prayer is like a burden (כְּמַשּׂוֹי)He wants only to be rid of it
The RabbisAnyone who does not say it in the language of supplicationRecitation without feeling
Rabba and Rav YosefAnyone unable to add something new (לְחַדֵּשׁ בָּהּ דָּבָר)Rabbi Zeira: I could add something new, but I fear I would become confused
Abaye bar Avin and Rabbi Ḥanina bar AvinAnyone who does not pray with the reddening of the sun (דִּמְדּוּמֵי חַמָּה)Rabbi Yoḥanan: it is a mitzva; Rabbi Zeira: 'let them fear You with the sun and before the moon' (Psalms 72:5)
But in the West: In Eretz Yisrael they cursed one who prays with the reddening of the sun before sunset — he may be distracted, lose track of the hour, and miss the time for prayer altogether.

🛣️ Brief Prayers for Danger and the Traveler's Prayer

The mishna: one walking in a place of danger says a short prayer 'at every parashat ha'ibur'
1️⃣ What is פָּרָשַׁת הָעִבּוּר? Rav Ḥisda in Mar Ukva's name: even when You are filled with anger (עֶבְרָה) at them like a pregnant woman (עוּבָּרָה) — or, even when they transgress (עוֹבְרִים) the Torah — may their needs be before You
↓
2️⃣ The Tosefta's brief prayers for a place of wild beasts and robbers: Rabbi Eliezer — 'Do Your will in heaven above and give calm to those who fear You below'; Rabbi Yehoshua — 'Hear the cry of Your people Israel'; Rabbi Elazar son of Rabbi Tzadok — 'Hear the shout of Your people'
↓
3️⃣ Aḥerim: 'The needs of Your people Israel are many and their understanding is short… give each one his sustenance and each body what it lacks.' Rav Huna: the halakha follows Aḥerim
↓
4️⃣ Elijah to Rav Yehuda, brother of Rav Sala Ḥasida: do not get angry and you will not sin; do not get drunk and you will not sin; and when you set out on the road, consult your Creator and go
↓
5️⃣ Rabbi Ya'akov in Rav Ḥisda's name: 'consult your Creator' is the traveler's prayer (תְּפִלַּת הַדֶּרֶךְ), and whoever sets out must recite it
↓
6️⃣ The text: lead me in peace, direct my steps in peace, support me in peace, save me from every enemy and ambush, bless the work of my hands, grant me grace, kindness, and mercy in Your eyes and in the eyes of all who see me — Blessed… Who hears prayer
All end alike: Every brief formula, and the traveler's prayer, closes with the blessing 'Who listens to prayer' (שׁוֹמֵעַ תְּפִלָּה).

📋 Berakhot 29 — Summary Map

🕯️ Shmuel HaKatan forgets the blessing he wrote → not removed, because he had begun it
↓
❓ Can a good person turn wicked? Abaye: no (Yannai = Yoḥanan); Rava: yes (Yoḥanan the High Priest)

↓
🎓 Key Principle: in danger, the short prayer of Aḥerim; on the road, 'consult your Creator' — the traveler's prayer is an obligation
