Berakhot 29 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
What is the Gemara's final explanation for why Shmuel HaKatan was not removed as prayer leader when he forgot the blessing of the heretics?
A. Because a good person never becomes wicked
B. Because he had already begun the blessing, and one who began may finish it
C. Because the congregation had no replacement
D. Because the blessing was not yet obligatory
Correct: B — After Rava's view leaves open that even the initially righteous can change, the Gemara answers that Shmuel HaKatan had begun reciting the blessing. Rav (or Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi) taught that removal applies only to one who did not begin it.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to Rabba and Rav Yosef, what makes a prayer 'fixed' (keva)?
A. Praying at the same hour every day
B. Praying without facing the Temple
C. Being unable to introduce something new into it
D. Praying after the reddening of the sun
Correct: C — Rabba and Rav Yosef define keva as the inability to add a novel, personal element. Rabbi Zeira remarked that he could do so but feared becoming confused, so this is not demanded of everyone.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why may Havineinu not be recited in the evening prayer at the conclusion of Shabbat?
A. Because it is too short to be said at night
B. Because havdala must be recited in the blessing 'Who graciously grants knowledge'
C. Because Abaye cursed anyone who says it
D. Because the request for rain must be included
Correct: B — Rav Naḥman in Shmuel's name excludes motzaei Shabbat and Festivals because havdala belongs in 'Who graciously grants knowledge', which Havineinu replaces. Mar Zutra's proposal to fold havdala into Havineinu was left unanswered (kashya).
QUESTION 4 OF 5
An individual forgot the request for rain in 'the blessing of the years' and remembered before reaching 'Who listens to prayer'. What does he do?
A. He returns to the beginning of the Amida
B. He returns to 'the blessing of the years'
C. He inserts the request in 'Who listens to prayer' and continues
D. He omits it and makes up for it in the next prayer
Correct: C — The Gemara resolves the contradiction between Rabbi Tanḥum and the Tosefta by timing: if he remembers before 'Who listens to prayer', he asks there; if only after it, he must return to the beginning.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
Whose version of the brief prayer for a place of danger does Rav Huna rule is the halakha?
A. Rabbi Eliezer's: 'Do Your will in the heavens above'
B. Rabbi Yehoshua's: 'Hear the cry of Your people Israel'
C. Rabbi Elazar son of Rabbi Tzadok's: 'Hear the shout of Your people'
D. Aḥerim's: 'The needs of Your people Israel are many and their understanding is short'
Correct: D — Rav Huna rules in accordance with Aḥerim, whose formula asks God to give each person his sustenance and each body what it lacks, ending 'Blessed are You, Lord, Who listens to prayer'.
