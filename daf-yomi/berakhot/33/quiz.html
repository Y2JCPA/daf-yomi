Berakhot 33 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Why does Rav Sheshet say one interrupts prayer for a scorpion but not for a snake?
A. A scorpion's sting is always fatal while a snake's is not
B. A snake will not bite unless it is attacked, but a scorpion stings even when undisturbed
C. Scorpions are more common in Babylonia than snakes
D. The mishna explicitly mentions scorpions
Correct: B — The mishna's 'snake wrapped around his heel' is limited to a snake, which does not bite if left alone; a scorpion is liable to sting regardless, so it is a real danger and one stops.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to Rabbi Yoḥanan, why was havdala at one point moved from the Amida to the cup of wine?
A. Because the Amida was too long
B. Because the people became wealthy and could afford wine
C. Because Rabbi Akiva ruled it must be a separate blessing
D. Because kiddush was moved at the same time
Correct: B — Initially havdala was fixed in the Amida; when the people became wealthy it was fixed over the cup; when they became poor again it returned to the Amida — and they ruled that one who says it in the Amida must also say it over the cup.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why is Rabbi Akiva's independent havdala blessing not adopted even on a festival that follows Shabbat?
A. Because Rabbi Akiva later retracted
B. Because the festival Amida has no blessing of knowledge
C. Because the Sages instituted seven festival blessings, not eight, just as they instituted eighteen weekday blessings, not nineteen
D. Because havdala over the cup replaces it
Correct: C — Just as throughout the year we do not follow Rabbi Akiva since the Sages fixed eighteen blessings and not nineteen, on a festival they fixed seven and not eight, so his view is set aside.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
What are the two reasons given for silencing one who says 'Your mercy reaches the bird's nest'?
A. It mentions a Torah law in prayer, and it addresses God in the second person
B. It casts jealousy among the works of Creation, and it presents God's attributes as mercy rather than decrees
C. It is too long, and it is not in Hebrew
D. It resembles a Christian prayer, and it repeats a word twice
Correct: B — Rabbi Yosei bar Avin and Rabbi Yosei bar Zevida disagree: one says it engenders jealousy among God's creatures, as if He favors one over another; the other says it turns God's decrees into expressions of mercy.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What was Rabbi Ḥanina's point in comparing God to a king with many thousands of gold dinars who is praised for silver ones?
A. That praise of God should be recited only in the Temple
B. That heaping up adjectives belittles God, since even 'great, mighty, awesome' are permitted only because Moses said them and the Great Assembly fixed them
C. That one should praise God with wealth rather than words
D. That silver blessings are preferable to golden ones
Correct: B — After a leader added 'powerful, strong, fearless, steadfast, honored', Rabbi Ḥanina objected that any list of praises falls infinitely short, like praising a fabulously rich king for his silver; we say only the three Moses said and the Great Assembly instituted.
