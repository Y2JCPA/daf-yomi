Berakhot 12 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Why does the Gemara reject the proof from 'Who forms light / Who brings on evenings' for the wine-and-beer dilemma?
A. Because the light blessing is recited only in the Temple
B. Because those are long blessings that end with their own concluding blessing, unlike a short food blessing
C. Because Rav holds that a blessing without God's name is no blessing
D. Because the baraita's rule applies only to the evening prayer
Correct: B — The Gemara answers 'it is different there, since one says Blessed… Who forms the lights' — a full closing blessing. A short blessing like shehakol has no such independent conclusion, so the case cannot be compared.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What did Rav Ḥisda and Rav Ashi say when Rabba bar bar Ḥana and Ameimar sought to institute daily recitation of the Ten Commandments?
A. That it should be done only in the Temple
B. That it had already been abolished because of the grievance of the heretics
C. That it would burden the congregation
D. That Moses did not divide that portion
Correct: B — Shmuel and Rabbi Natan report that the practice outside the Temple was abolished due to the heretics' grievance, and both later attempts in Sura and Neharde'a were stopped with the same answer.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to Rav, when does one bow and when does one straighten up during prayer?
A. Bow at God's name, straighten at 'Blessed'
B. Bow at 'Blessed', straighten at God's name
C. Bow at the beginning of every blessing and straighten at its end
D. Bow only at the conclusion of the Amida
Correct: B — Rabba bar Ḥinnana Sava in the name of Rav: he bows at Barukh and straightens at the Name. Shmuel explains from 'The Lord raises the bowed' (Psalms 146:8), and 'before My name he was afraid' teaches humility before, not at, the Name.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why did the Sages ultimately not include the portion of Balak in Shema, despite the verse 'He couched, He lay down like a lion'?
A. Because it does not mention the Exodus from Egypt
B. Because Balaam was a gentile prophet
C. Because one may not read a single verse from a portion Moses did not divide, and the whole portion would burden the congregation
D. Because it was replaced by the Ten Commandments
Correct: C — The Gemara says reading only that verse is impossible — 'a portion Moses did not divide, we do not divide' — and reciting the entire portion was rejected because of the burden on the congregation (טוֹרַח צִבּוּר).
QUESTION 5 OF 5
How does Ben Zoma derive that the Exodus must be mentioned at night?
A. From 'when you lie down and when you rise'
B. From the extra word 'all' in 'all the days of your life' (Deut. 16:3)
C. From Jeremiah's prophecy about the north country
D. From the portion of tzitzit, which mentions the Exodus
Correct: B — Ben Zoma: 'the days of your life' would mean daytime; 'all the days of your life' adds the nights. The Sages instead use 'all' to include the days of the Messiah.
