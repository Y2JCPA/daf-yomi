Berakhot 46 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Rabbi Yoḥanan in the name of Rabbi Shimon ben Yoḥai, how are the honors divided at a meal?
A. The host both breaks bread and recites Grace after Meals
B. The guest both breaks bread and recites Grace after Meals
C. The host breaks bread and the guest recites Grace after Meals
D. The eldest person present does both
Correct: C — The host breaks bread so that he will break generously, and the guest recites Grace after Meals so that he can bless his host in the course of it.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What is the core of the blessing the guest recites for his host?
A. That the host's table always be full of guests
B. That the host not suffer shame in this world nor humiliation in the World-to-Come
C. That the host merit children who study Torah
D. That the host be repaid double for what he spent
Correct: B — That is the base formula; Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi added requests for material success, for possessions near the city, and for protection from Satan and from thoughts of sin.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What is the dispute between Rav Naḥman and Rav Sheshet on this daf?
A. Whether a zimmun requires three or ten men
B. How far the zimmun blessing extends — until 'Let us bless' or until the end of 'Who feeds all'
C. Whether a guest may recite Grace after Meals at all
D. Whether laborers must recite Grace after Meals
Correct: B — Rav Naḥman limits the zimmun blessing to 'Let us bless'; Rav Sheshet extends it through the first blessing of Grace after Meals, 'Who feeds all'.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Which of these is NOT one of the daf's proofs that 'Who is good and does good' is rabbinic rather than Torah law?
A. Laborers eliminate it when they recite Grace at work
B. It opens with 'Barukh' but does not close with 'Barukh'
C. It is reworked in the house of the mourner
D. It is omitted on Shabbat and festivals
Correct: D — Rav Yosef, Rav Yitzḥak bar Shmuel bar Marta in Rav's name, and Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak each bring one of the first three proofs. Nothing on the daf says the blessing is omitted on Shabbat or festivals.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What did Rabbi Ḥiyya explain to Rav when Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi told him at a meal, 'Stand, wash your hands'?
A. That Rav had eaten something impure and needed to purify himself
B. That Rav was being asked to serve the water to the other guests
C. That being first to wash after the meal meant Rav was being honored to lead Grace after Meals
D. That the meal was over and Rav should leave
Correct: C — Rav trembled, fearing he had behaved improperly. Rabbi Ḥiyya reassured him: 'Son of noblemen, he is telling you to review Grace after Meals,' since whoever washes first at the end of the meal is designated to bless.
