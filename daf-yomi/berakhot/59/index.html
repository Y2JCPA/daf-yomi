📖 Overview: Blessings on the Forces of Nature

Berakhot 59a–59b
וְעַל הַבְּרָקִים אוֹמֵר ״בָּרוּךְ … שֶׁכֹּחוֹ וּגְבוּרָתוֹ מָלֵא עוֹלָם״
The scene: The mishna assigns the blessing 'Whose strength and power fill the world' to earthquakes, thunder, winds and lightning, and 'Author of creation' to mountains, hills, seas, rivers and deserts. The Gemara works through each item: it finishes the story of the stars taken from Pleiades for the flood, explains earthquakes through Rav Ketina's encounter with a necromancer, and offers competing physical explanations for thunder. It then turns to the rainbow, the clear firmament, the sun's cycle, the Euphrates and Tigris, and finally the blessing on rain, which forces a precise definition of when one says HaTov veHaMeitiv rather than Sheheḥeyanu.

⚡ 'Whose Strength Fills the World' — What Are These Phenomena?

The Gemara asks 'what is it?' and 'why does it happen?' for each item in the mishna
PhenomenonWhat is it?Why does it happen?
Earthquake (זְוָעוֹת)Rav Ketina: a rumbling of the earth (guha)The necromancer: God sheds two tears into the great sea for His suffering children. Rav Ketina: God claps His hands (Ezek. 21:22). R' Natan: God sighs (Ezek. 5:13). The Rabbis: God kicks the firmament (Jer. 25:30). Rav Aḥa bar Ya'akov: God presses His feet beneath the throne of glory (Isa. 66:1)
Thunder (רְעָמִים)A sound from the cloudsShmuel: clouds colliding with the curve of the firmament (Ps. 77:19). The Rabbis: clouds pouring water into one another (Jer. 10:13). Rav Aḥa bar Ya'akov: a strong bolt of lightning shattering hailstones. Rav Ashi: wind blowing across hollow clouds like across the mouth of a jug. Conclusion: Rav Aḥa bar Ya'akov — lightning flashes, clouds rumble, rain comes
Winds (רוּחוֹת)Abaye: a gale (za'afa)Tradition: a gale never begins at night, and never lasts two hours — 'trouble shall not rise up a second time' (Nahum 1:9). If it seems longer, it paused in between
Lightning (בְּרָקִים)Rava: a bright flashRava: a single bolt, white lightning, green lightning, clouds rising in the west and coming from the south, or two clouds facing each other — all are signs of trouble, so pray for mercy. Only at night; in the morning they mean nothing
Why thunder exists: R' Alexandri in the name of R' Yehoshua ben Levi: thunder was created only to straighten the crookedness of the heart — 'God has so made it that men should fear before Him' (Eccl. 3:14). On morning clouds: R' Shmuel bar Yitzḥak says they have no substance (Hosea 6:4), but the folk saying 'rain when the doors open — donkey-driver, fold your sack and sleep' is about thick clouds, not flimsy ones.

🌈 The Rainbow: Fall on Your Face? And Which Blessing?

Ezekiel saw the glory of God 'as the appearance of the bow in the cloud'
R' Yehoshua b. Levi
One who sees a rainbow in a cloud must fall on his face, as Ezekiel says: 'As the appearance of the bow that is in the cloud… when I saw it, I fell upon my face' (Ezek. 1:28).
The West
In Eretz Yisrael they cursed anyone who did that — it looks like he is bowing down to the rainbow itself.
Gemara
Whatever one does with his face, all agree that he certainly recites a blessing. Which one?
Answer
בָּרוּךְ … זוֹכֵר הַבְּרִית — 'Blessed… Who remembers the covenant' made with Noah.
Baraita
R' Yishmael, son of R' Yoḥanan ben Beroka, says the blessing is נֶאֱמָן בִּבְרִיתוֹ וְקַיָּים בְּמַאֲמָרוֹ — 'Who is faithful to His covenant and fulfills His word'.
Rav Pappa
Therefore we say them both: 'Blessed… Who remembers the covenant, and is faithful to His covenant and fulfills His word'.
Rav Pappa's method: When two formulas compete, Rav Pappa merges them. He does it again on this daf with the rain blessing, ruling that we say both 'abundant thanksgivings' (רוֹב הַהוֹדָאוֹת) and Rava's 'the God of thanksgivings' (הָאֵל הַהוֹדָאוֹת).

🏔️ 'Author of Creation' — Over What, and How Many Blessings?

Mountains, the clear sky, the sun's cycle and the great rivers

What gets 'Oseh Ma'aseh Bereishit'

  • Mountains and hills (the mishna)
  • The sun at the start of its cycle — Abaye: every 28 years, when the Nisan equinox falls in Saturn on the night between the third and fourth days of the week
  • The moon in its might, the planets in their orbits, the zodiac in its order (baraita)
  • The Euphrates at the bridge of Babylonia — but since the Persians rerouted it, only from Beit Shavor upriver (Rav Yosef: from Ihi Dekira upriver)
  • The Tigris at the bridge of Shabistana (Rami bar Abba)

One blessing or two?

  • Question: aren't lightning and thunder also acts of creation? 'He makes lightning for the rain' (Ps. 135:7)
  • Abaye: combine the two clauses — over every item in the mishna recite both blessings
  • Rava: over thunder and lightning say two — 'Whose power fills the world' and 'Author of creation'; over mountains and hills, only 'Author of creation'

The firmament in its purity

  • R' Yehoshua ben Levi: one who sees the sky in its purity recites 'Author of creation'
  • Abaye: that is when rain fell all night and a north wind exposed the heavens in the morning
  • Rav Ḥisda (via Rafram bar Pappa) disagrees: since the Temple was destroyed the sky has never been seen in its purity — 'I clothe the heavens with blackness' (Isa. 50:3)
Also on the daf: R' Yehuda's 'one who sees the great sea intermittently' means after thirty days (Rami bar Abba citing Rav Yitzḥak). The river names are explained: Ḥidekel because its waters are sharp (ḥadin) and light (kalin); Perat because its waters are fruitful (parin) and multiply (ravin). Rava adds that the people of Meḥoza are sharp because they drink Tigris water.

🌧️ Which Blessing on Rain? Three Attempts and a Resolution

The mishna says HaTov veHaMeitiv, but R' Abbahu's tradition gives a different text
❓ Mishna: over rain one says הַטּוֹב וְהַמֵּטִיב. But R' Abbahu (or a baraita) says the blessing on rain begins 'when the groom goes out to meet the bride' — raindrops splashing the puddles — and its text is different
↓
📜 Rav Yehuda's text: 'We thank You for each and every drop You have made fall for us' (מוֹדִים אֲנַחְנוּ לָךְ עַל כׇּל טִפָּה וְטִפָּה). R' Yoḥanan closes with 'If our mouths were as full of song as the sea…' and ends 'to Whom abundant thanksgivings are offered'
↓
Attempt 1
Mishna = one who heard rain fell; R' Abbahu = one who saw it. Rejected: hearing is simply good tidings, which the mishna lists separately
Attempt 2
Both saw it: a little rain → 'We thank You'; a lot of rain → HaTov veHaMeitiv
Attempt 3
Both a lot of rain: one who owns land → HaTov veHaMeitiv; no land → 'We thank You'
↓
❓ But a landowner should say שֶׁהֶחֱיָינוּ! The mishna itself says: a new house or vessels → Sheheḥeyanu; something belonging to him and others → HaTov veHaMeitiv
↓
✅ Resolution: land owned in partnership → HaTov veHaMeitiv; land exclusively his → Sheheḥeyanu. As the baraita sums up: 'For what is his alone — Sheheḥeyanu; for his and his fellow's — HaTov veHaMeitiv'
Rov hahoda'ot: Why 'abundant thanksgivings' and not 'all thanksgivings'? Rava: say 'the God of thanksgivings'. Rav Pappa: say both.

🤝 Testing the Partnership Principle

Three baraitot seem to give HaTov veHaMeitiv to a person who benefits alone
1️⃣ Principle: הַטּוֹב וְהַמֵּטִיב is said only when someone else shares the benefit; שֶׁהֶחֱיָינוּ when the good is his alone
↓
2️⃣ Challenge: 'They told him his wife bore a son — he says HaTov veHaMeitiv.' Answer: his wife is with him; she too is glad of a son
↓
3️⃣ Challenge: 'His father died and he inherits — first דַּיַּין הָאֱמֶת, then HaTov veHaMeitiv.' Answer: it speaks of a case where brothers inherit along with him
↓
4️⃣ Challenge: a change of wine needs no new 'fruit of the vine', yet R' Yoḥanan (via R' Yosef bar Abba) says one recites HaTov veHaMeitiv. Answer: the members of the group are drinking with him
↓
5️⃣ Conclusion: every case of HaTov veHaMeitiv involves another person who benefits — the principle stands
New house, new vessels: Rav Huna: Sheheḥeyanu was taught only when one has nothing similar already; if he owns a similar item (say, inherited), no blessing. R' Yoḥanan: even if he owns a similar item he blesses, because he never before bought one. The Gemara continues this dispute on the next daf.

📋 Berakhot 59 — Summary Map

⭐ Pleiades lost two stars to the flood; God patched it with Ursa Major's stars — a prosecutor cannot become an advocate
↓
🌍 Earthquake, thunder, wind, lightning → 'Whose strength fills the world'; thunder is best explained as lightning shattering hail (Rav Aḥa bar Ya'akov)
↓
🌈 Rainbow → 'Who remembers the covenant, faithful to His covenant and fulfills His word' (Rav Pappa says both); do not fall on your face in the West
↓
🏔️ Mountains, clear sky, sun every 28 years, Euphrates and Tigris → 'Author of creation' (Rava: thunder gets both blessings, mountains only one)
🌧️ Rain: 'We thank You for each drop' — or HaTov veHaMeitiv when land is owned in partnership
↓
🎓 Key Principle: HaTov veHaMeitiv requires a partner in the good — a wife, brothers, or fellow diners; what is yours alone earns Sheheḥeyanu
