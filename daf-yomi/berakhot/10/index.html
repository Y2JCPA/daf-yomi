📖 Overview: Berurya, David's Songs, and Hezekiah's Prayer

Berakhot 10a–10b
אֲפִילּוּ חֶרֶב חַדָּה מוּנַּחַת עַל צַוָּארוֹ שֶׁל אָדָם, אַל יִמְנַע עַצְמוֹ מִן הָרַחֲמִים
The scene: The daf opens with the proof that Psalms 1 and 2 are one chapter, then turns to Berurya, who corrected her husband Rabbi Meir's prayer against hooligans and silenced a heretic. Rabbi Abbahu defends the method of learning from juxtaposed verses. Rabbi Shimon ben Yoḥai reads Proverbs 31:26 as praise of David, who sang 'Bless the Lord, O my soul' in five worlds, and the Gemara draws five parallels between the soul and God. Then comes the story of Hezekiah and Isaiah: God brought illness on the king to make the two meet, Isaiah decreed his death for not having children, and Hezekiah refused to give up — 'even with a sharp sword on his neck, do not refrain from praying for mercy'. Amud bet lists Hezekiah's six deeds, explains why relying on one's own merit backfires, and describes the woman of Shunem's chamber for Elisha. It closes with rules of prayer posture, Shema after the third hour, and the mishna of Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel on how to recite Shema.

🕊️ Berurya Corrects Rabbi Meir

Two exchanges that hinge on reading a verse to its end
Gemara
Hooligans in Rabbi Meir's neighborhood tormented him. He prayed that they should die.
Berurya
What are you thinking? Because it says יִתַּמּוּ חַטָּאִים (Psalms 104:35)? Is it written sinners (חוֹטְאִים)? It is written sins! And look at the end of the verse: 'and the wicked will be no more' — pray that they repent, and there will be no more wicked.
Gemara
He prayed for mercy on them, and they repented.
Heretic
'Sing, barren woman who has not given birth' (Isaiah 54:1) — because she has not given birth she should sing?
Berurya
Fool! Go to the end of the verse: 'for more are the children of the desolate than the children of the married wife'. The congregation of Israel sings because, like a barren woman, she did not bear children destined for Gehenna — like you.
R' Abbahu
To a heretic asking why David's flight from Absalom (Psalm 3) is written before his flight from Saul (Psalm 57): we expound juxtaposition — 'adjoined forever and ever' (Psalms 111:8). Absalom sits beside Gog and Magog to teach: just as a son can rebel against his father, so people can rebel against their Master.
Method: Both of Berurya's answers use the same tool — שְׁפֵיל לְסֵיפֵיהּ דִּקְרָא, 'go down to the end of the verse'.

🎵 David's Five Worlds and the Soul's Five Likenesses to God

'She opens her mouth with wisdom' (Proverbs 31:26) — Solomon said it of his father David
WorldDavid's songParallel: just as God… so the soul…
In his mother's womb'Bless the Lord, O my soul, and all that is within me' (Psalms 103:1)…fills the whole world / fills the whole body
Emerging and seeing the stars'Bless the Lord, His angels… all His hosts' (Psalms 103:20–23)…sees but is not seen / sees but is not seen
Nursing at his mother's breast'Do not forget all His benefits' (Psalms 103:2) — Rabbi Abbahu: He placed her breasts at the place of understanding…sustains the whole world / sustains the whole body
Seeing the downfall of the wicked'Let sinners cease… Halleluya' (Psalms 104:35)…is pure / is pure
Looking upon the day of death'Lord my God, You are very great' (Psalms 104:1) — proven by 'You hide Your face, they perish' (104:29)…dwells in the innermost chambers / dwells in the innermost chambers
Rav Shimi bar Ukva before Rabbi Shimon ben Pazi: Flesh and blood paints a form on a wall but cannot give it spirit, soul, and innards; God forms a form within a form and gives it all these — Hannah's 'there is no Rock (צוּר) like our God' means no Artist (צַיָּיר) like our God. And 'there is none like You' — read 'none can outlast You': man's works outlast him, but God outlasts His works. Let the one with these five qualities come and praise the One with these five qualities.

🗡️ Hezekiah and Isaiah: A Sword on the Neck

Rav Hamnuna: 'Who knows the interpretation (pesher) of the matter' — God makes compromise (peshara) between two righteous men
❓ Hezekiah: let Isaiah come to me, as Elijah went to Ahab. Isaiah: let Hezekiah come to me, as Yehoram went to Elisha
↓
🤒 God's solution: He made Hezekiah ill and told Isaiah, 'go visit the sick'. Isaiah: 'you will die and you will not live' (Isaiah 38:1) — die in this world, no life in the World-to-Come
↓
❓ Why? Because you did not engage in procreation. Hezekiah: I saw by divine inspiration that my children would not be virtuous. Isaiah: 'Why do you involve yourself with the secrets of the Merciful One? Do what you are commanded, and let God do what pleases Him'
↓
Isaiah
Hezekiah asks for his daughter in marriage; Isaiah: the decree is already sealed
Hezekiah
'Son of Amoz, finish your prophecy and leave! I have a tradition from my father's father: even with a sharp sword on a man's neck, he should not refrain from praying for mercy'
↓
✅ Rabbi Yoḥanan and Rabbi Eliezer agree — 'Though He slay me, I will trust in Him' (Job 13:15). Rabbi Ḥanan: even if the master of dreams says you die tomorrow, keep praying (Ecclesiastes 5:6). 'Hezekiah turned his face to the wall (קִיר) and prayed' — Reish Lakish: from the chambers of his heart; Rabbi Levi: recalling the Shunammite's small wall — if You revived her son, how much more for a descendant of Solomon who plated the whole Sanctuary in gold
'What was good in Your eyes I did': Rav Yehuda in the name of Rav: he juxtaposed redemption to prayer. Rabbi Levi: he hid away the Book of Remedies.

👑 Hezekiah's Six Deeds, and Whose Merit to Invoke

The Sages agreed with three and disagreed with three

Agreed

  • Hid away the Book of Remedies
  • Ground up the copper serpent
  • Dragged his father Ahaz's bones on a bed of ropes

Did not agree

  • Stopped up the waters of the Gihon
  • Cut off the Sanctuary doors and sent them to the king of Assyria
  • Intercalated Nisan in Nisan — 'this month shall be for you the first' (Exodus 12:2): no other month is Nisan. Rather, he erred like Shmuel's rule: one may not intercalate on the 30th of Adar since it is fit to be Rosh Ḥodesh Nisan; Hezekiah held we do not say 'since it is fit'

Own merit vs. others' merit

  • Rabbi Yoḥanan in the name of Rabbi Yosei ben Zimra: rely on your own merit and you are answered through others'; rely on others' merit and you are answered through your own
  • Moses invoked 'Abraham, Isaac and Israel' (Exodus 32:13) → answered by his own: 'had Moses His chosen not stood in the breach' (Psalms 106:23)
  • Hezekiah invoked 'remember that I walked before You' (Isaiah 38:3) → answered 'for My sake and for the sake of David My servant' (II Kings 19:34)
  • Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi: 'for my peace I had great bitterness' (Isaiah 38:17) — even the peace God sent him was bitter

🏠 The Shunammite's Chamber and the Rules of Prayer

From 'let us make a small walled upper chamber' (II Kings 4:10) to Beit Shammai and Beit Hillel
1️⃣ Rav and Shmuel: it was an open upper story they roofed (kir = ceiling), or a large veranda they divided in two (aliyat = the finest of rooms)
↓
2️⃣ Abaye (or Rabbi Yitzḥak): one who wishes to benefit from hosts may, like Elisha; one who does not, need not, like Samuel of Rama, whose house went with him everywhere
↓
3️⃣ Rabbi Yosei son of Rabbi Ḥanina: 'I perceive that he is a holy man of God' — a woman recognizes guests better than a man. How did she know? Rav and Shmuel: no fly passed over his table, or no stain on the linen sheet. 'He' is holy — but his attendant Geihazi, who grabbed her, is not
↓
4️⃣ Rabbi Eliezer ben Ya'akov: one who hosts a Torah scholar and lets him enjoy his possessions is as if he offered the daily tamid — 'passes by us continually (תָּמִיד)'
↓
5️⃣ Also from Rabbi Eliezer ben Ya'akov: pray from a low place, not a high one — 'from the depths I called You' (Psalms 130:1); align your feet like the angels' 'straight foot' (Ezekiel 1:7); do not eat before praying — 'you shall not eat with the blood' (Leviticus 19:26), 'Me you have cast behind your pride'
↓
6️⃣ Rav Yehuda in the name of Shmuel: the halakha follows Rabbi Yehoshua — morning Shema until three hours. After that one 'loses nothing': the baraita rules he still says two blessings before and one after, refuting Rav Ḥisda's first version. Rabbi Mani: reciting Shema in its time is greater than Torah study
New mishna: Beit Shammai: in the evening recline and recite, in the morning stand — 'when you lie down and when you rise'. Beit Hillel: recite as you are — 'when you walk on the way'; the verse only marks the times people lie down and rise. Rabbi Tarfon reclined on the road like Beit Shammai and was endangered by bandits; the Sages told him: you deserved to forfeit your life for transgressing the words of Beit Hillel.
