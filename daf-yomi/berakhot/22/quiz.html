Berakhot 22 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak, how can Rabbi Yehuda permit a ba'al keri to recite the blessings aloud?
A. Rabbi Yehuda holds that contemplation is like speech
B. Rabbi Yehuda treats the blessings like Hilkhot Derekh Eretz, which a ba'al keri may study
C. Rabbi Yehuda holds that Ezra's decree was never accepted
D. Rabbi Yehuda requires nine kav before any blessing
Correct: B — Since the baraita shows Rabbi Yehuda rejects contemplation as a solution, Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak explains that he classifies the blessings with Hilkhot Derekh Eretz, the one area Rabbi Yehuda permits a ba'al keri to study.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What verse does Rabbi Yehuda ben Beteira cite to prove that words of Torah do not become ritually impure?
A. 'And you shall impart them to your children' (Deuteronomy 4:9)
B. 'Is not My word like fire, says the Lord' (Jeremiah 23:29)
C. 'You shall not sow your vineyard with diverse kinds' (Deuteronomy 22:9)
D. 'The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination' (Proverbs 21:27)
Correct: B — Just as fire cannot become impure, so words of Torah cannot; on this basis he told the hesitant student to open his mouth and let his words illuminate.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why, according to one version, did ben Azzai only whisper the nine-kav leniency to his students?
A. Because it was a secret of the Ma'aseh Merkava
B. So that Torah scholars would not be with their wives like roosters
C. Because Rabbi Akiva forbade him to teach it
D. Because nine kav applies only to the sick
Correct: B — The version 'he taught it openly' aims to prevent neglect of Torah and procreation; the version 'he whispered it' fears that easy purification would lead scholars to excessive marital relations, distracting from study.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
What is Rava's final ruling on purification after a seminal emission?
A. Everyone needs forty se'a, healthy or sick
B. Healthy people need nine kav; sick people are exempt
C. Normal emission (healthy or sick) needs forty se'a; a healthy person's involuntary emission needs nine kav; a sick person's involuntary emission needs nothing
D. Nine kav suffices for everyone, provided it is poured rather than immersed in
Correct: C — Rava rules in accordance with the first amoraic opinion on the sick: a sick person with a normal emission is like a healthy one, but a sick person with an involuntary emission is exempt from any purification.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
One who prayed and afterward discovered feces in the place where he prayed — what does Rava rule?
A. His prayer is valid because he did not know
B. He need only step four cubits away and continue
C. His prayer is an abomination, as 'the sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination', and he must pray again
D. He returns to the point where he stopped
Correct: C — Rabba held that although he sinned by not checking, his prayer is valid; Rava objected from Proverbs 21:27 and ruled that because he sinned, his prayer is an abomination.
