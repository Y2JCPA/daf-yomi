Berakhot 48 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to the Gemara's conclusion, which minor is included in a zimmun?
A. Only a minor who has grown two hairs
B. A minor lying in a cradle
C. A minor who knows to Whom one recites a blessing
D. Any minor whose father is present
Correct: C — The Gemara rejects the earlier statements and rules like Rav Naḥman: a minor who knows to Whom we bless is counted.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why did Rabbi Yirmeya say Rabbi Zeira 'did well' not to ask about six grain-eaters out of ten?
A. Because six is obviously too few to form any zimmun
B. Because six is still a majority, so the answer is obviously that they join
C. Because the question had already been decided by Rav
D. Because vegetables never count toward a zimmun
Correct: B — Rabbi Yirmeya reasoned that seven and eight work because they are a majority, and six is also a majority. Rabbi Zeira, by contrast, required an obvious majority and therefore considered six insufficient.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What does Rabbi Yoḥanan say about Shimon ben Shataḥ reciting Grace after drinking a cup of wine at King Yannai's table?
A. He acted correctly; drinking wine is enough to lead Grace
B. He acted on his own; one cannot discharge others' obligation without eating an olive-bulk of grain
C. He should not have joined the meal at all because Yannai had killed the Sages
D. He should have eaten a dry fig rather than drinking wine
Correct: B — Rabbi Yoḥanan holds that a token participation lets one join the zimmun, but to fulfill the obligation on behalf of others one must eat an olive-bulk of grain. Shimon ben Shataḥ's conduct was his own, not the accepted halakha.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why was the blessing 'Who is good and does good' instituted at Yavne?
A. To thank God for the manna
B. To mark Solomon's completion of the Temple
C. Because the slain of Beitar did not decompose and were finally brought to burial
D. To commemorate Israel's entry into the land
Correct: C — Rav Mattana: on the day the slain of Beitar were brought to burial, the Sages at Yavne instituted 'Who is good' — their bodies had not decomposed — 'and does good' — they were brought to burial.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
How does Rabbi Yishmael derive the obligation to recite a blessing over the Torah?
A. From the word 'gave' used both for the land and for the tablets
B. From 'tova' in 'the good land', which alludes to Torah
C. By a fortiori inference: if one blesses over food, which is temporal life, all the more so over Torah, which is eternal life
D. From the verse 'Praise God with me'
Correct: C — Rabbi Yishmael uses a kal vaḥomer from Grace after Meals. His student Rabbi Ḥiyya bar Naḥmani offers the natan/ve'etna parallel, and Rabbi Yehuda ben Beteira the reading of 'tova' as Torah.
