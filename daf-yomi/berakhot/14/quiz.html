QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Rabba, when may an individual interrupt Hallel to greet someone?
A. Never, because publicizing the miracle takes precedence
B. Only on days when the whole Hallel is completed, and even in the middle of a paragraph
C. On full-Hallel days only between paragraphs; on days when Hallel is not completed, even in the middle of a paragraph
D. Only during the Megilla, not during Hallel
Correct: C — Rabba distinguishes days when the individual completes Hallel (interrupt only between paragraphs) from days when he does not (interrupt even mid-paragraph). Ravina's failure to greet Rav bar Shaba is explained by Rav bar Shaba's lack of importance in Ravina's eyes.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
How does Rabbi Abba reconcile Rav's statement about greeting before prayer with the mishna that permits greeting between paragraphs of Shema?
A. Rav's rule applies only on Shabbat
B. Rav's rule applies only to one who rises early and goes to another's door to greet him
C. Rav retracted after Shmuel's objection
D. The mishna refers to the evening Shema, which comes after prayer
Correct: B — Rav Sheshet raised the objection from the mishna, and Rabbi Abba explained that Rav prohibits specifically setting out early to greet someone at his door (בְּמַשְׁכִּים לְפִתְחוֹ); an incidental encounter is permitted.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What is Rabbi Yoḥanan's source for Rabbi Yehuda's rule that one may not interrupt between 'your God' and 'True and Firm'?
A. 'Hear, Israel, the Lord is our God, the Lord is One'
B. 'And the Lord God is True' (Jeremiah 10:10)
C. 'To declare Your faith in the nights' (Psalms 92:3)
D. 'Righteousness shall go before Him' (Psalms 85:14)
Correct: B — Rabbi Abbahu in Rabbi Yoḥanan's name links 'Hashem Eloheikhem Emet' to the verse 'And the Lord God is True', so the words must be recited together without interruption.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why did Abaye conclude that 'we begin and we also complete' the tzitzit portion at night?
A. Because the mitzva of tzitzit applies at night as well
B. Because Ḥiyya bar Rav required saying Emet after it
C. Because they begin it in the West, and Rav taught that once one begins the portion he must complete it
D. Because Rav Pappa ruled that the entire portion must always be recited
Correct: C — Abaye reasons that since Eretz Yisrael recites at least the opening of the portion, we begin it too; and Rav Kahana in Rav's name taught 'one should not begin, but if he began, he completes' — so we finish it.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What was the final explanation for Rav reciting Shema before donning tefillin?
A. Rav holds that acceptance of the kingdom of Heaven precedes all action
B. Rav had not yet reached the time for Shema
C. Rav was digging a grave and was exempt
D. The messenger was at fault and brought Rav's tefillin late
Correct: D — The appeal to Rabbi Yehoshua ben Korḥa is rejected (he spoke of recitation before recitation, not before action), and Rav Ḥiyya bar Ashi's testimony shows Rav normally donned tefillin first. The Gemara concludes: the messenger erred and delivered the tefillin late.
