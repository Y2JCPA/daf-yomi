Berakhot 43 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to the first version of Rav's statement, for which food does reclining allow one person to bless on behalf of all?
A. Bread only; wine does not require reclining
B. Wine only; bread never needs reclining
C. Both bread and wine equally
D. Neither; each person always blesses for himself
Correct: A — Rav limits the mishna's 'if they reclined, one blesses for all' to bread. Rabbi Yoḥanan holds that even wine requires reclining.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why, according to Ben Zoma, does each diner bless for himself when wine is served during the meal?
A. Because wine during the meal is not considered important
B. Because the throat is not free — diners are busy eating and cannot all be ready to drink together
C. Because reclining has not yet begun
D. Because wine during a meal is secondary to the bread
Correct: B — Ben Zoma explained: 'since the throat is not free' (אֵין בֵּית הַבְּלִיעָה פָּנוּי). After the meal, when all are free, one blesses for everyone.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Which blessing is recited over musk, and why?
A. 'Who creates fragrant trees', because it is treated like incense
B. 'Who creates fragrant plants', because it grows in the field
C. 'Who creates various spices', because it comes from a living creature
D. 'Who gave pleasant fragrance in fruits', because it is sweet
Correct: C — Rav Ḥisda's teaching: all incense gets 'fragrant trees' except musk, which is from a living creature, so its blessing is 'Who creates various spices' (בּוֹרֵא מִינֵי בְשָׂמִים).
QUESTION 4 OF 5
When oil and myrtle are brought together, how does Rabban Gamliel decide, and what is the halakha?
A. Myrtle first, since it is a plant; the halakha follows Beit Hillel
B. Oil first, since we enjoy both its scent and its anointing; Rabbi Yoḥanan rules that the halakha follows the decisor
C. Either order, since both are fragrances
D. Wine first, then oil, then myrtle
Correct: B — Rabban Gamliel sided with Beit Shammai because oil gives two benefits, scent and anointing, while myrtle gives only scent. Rabbi Yoḥanan: the halakha follows the decisor.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
From whom does the Gemara derive that one should rather throw himself into a fiery furnace than shame another in public?
A. From Tamar, who did not expose Judah when she was brought out to be burned
B. From Rav, who trembled when Rabbi told him to wash his hands
C. From Rav Pappa, who blessed the myrtle first
D. From the Torah scholar who may not go out perfumed
Correct: A — The teaching is derived from Tamar (Genesis 38:25): facing execution, she sent Judah the pledges and let him identify them rather than humiliate him publicly.
