Berakhot 55 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
How does the Gemara reconcile Rav Yehuda's praise for prolonging prayer with the teaching that prolonging prayer leads to heartache?
A. Prolonging is good only in the morning prayer
B. Prolonging is bad when one expects the prayer to be answered, and good when one merely increases supplication
C. Prolonging is good only for a Torah scholar
D. Prolonging is bad only on a fast day
Correct: B — The Gemara distinguishes between one who is 'me'ayen bah' — expects his prayer to be accepted — which leads to heartache, and one who simply adds supplication without such expectation.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to Rabbi Yoḥanan and Rabbi Elazar, what does a person's table accomplish now that the Temple is destroyed?
A. It substitutes for the Shabbat candles
B. It atones for him, as the altar once atoned for Israel
C. It obligates him to invite a Torah scholar
D. It replaces the cup of blessing
Correct: B — Ezekiel 41:22 begins with 'the altar' and ends with 'the table'; while the Temple stood the altar atoned, and now one's table atones — for example by feeding the poor who come during a meal.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What does Rabbi Yitzḥak derive from God asking Moses whether Bezalel was suitable in his eyes?
A. That a leader must be from the tribe of Judah
B. That a leader may be appointed over a community only if the community is consulted
C. That Moses had authority to veto divine appointments
D. That craftsmen require rabbinic ordination
Correct: B — God asked Moses, and then had Moses ask Israel, whether Bezalel was suitable — teaching that a communal leader is appointed only with the community's consent.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why does the Gemara reread Rabbi Yoḥanan's instruction that a distraught dreamer should 'interpret' his dream before three people?
A. Because three people cannot agree on an interpretation
B. Because interpreting a dream promotes its fulfillment, per Rav Ḥisda's 'unread letter' maxim, so he should instead 'better' it
C. Because only a professional interpreter may be consulted
D. Because the dream must first be reported to the priests
Correct: B — Since an uninterpreted dream is like an unread letter that cannot be fulfilled, one worried about a dream should not have it interpreted; rather, he should have three people declare it good and recite verses of transformation, redemption, and peace.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What caveat does Rava attach to the principle that 'all dreams follow the mouth' of the interpreter?
A. The interpreter must be paid
B. The interpretation must be given within three days
C. The interpretation must be akin to the dream, as Joseph interpreted 'each man according to his dream'
D. The interpreter must be one of the twenty-four in Jerusalem
Correct: C — Rava limits the principle to an interpretation that fits the dream's content, citing Genesis 41:12, 'each man according to his dream he did interpret'.
