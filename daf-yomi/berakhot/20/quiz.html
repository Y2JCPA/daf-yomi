Berakhot 20 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Why can't a general principle that human dignity overrides Torah law be derived from the Nazirite burying a met mitzva?
A. Because the Nazirite is not bound by purity laws
B. Because a case of 'sit and refrain from action' is different — he does not actively violate a mitzva
C. Because burial of a met mitzva is only rabbinic
D. Because the Nazirite's relatives may bury the body instead
Correct: B — Engaging in the burial does not directly contravene a mitzva; it only renders him unable to fulfill one. Such a shev ve'al ta'aseh case cannot teach that dignity overrides an active prohibition.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to Abaye, why did miracles occur for the earlier generations but not for Rav Pappa's generation?
A. Because they studied all six orders of the Mishna
B. Because they learned thirteen versions of Okatzin
C. Because they were wholly devoted to sanctifying God's name, and we are not
D. Because they fasted more often for rain
Correct: C — Rav Pappa noted that his generation learned far more than Rav Yehuda's, yet Rav Yehuda's removing one shoe brought rain. Abaye answered that the earlier generations were dedicated to sanctifying God's name.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why did the mishna need to state that women are exempt from Shema if it is obviously a time-bound positive mitzva?
A. Because Shema is juxtaposed to mezuza
B. Because Shema contains acceptance of the yoke of Heaven, so one might think women are obligated regardless
C. Because women were obligated in Shema by an earlier decree
D. Because minors are obligated in Shema
Correct: B — The Gemara says: lest you say that since Shema includes acceptance of the kingdom of Heaven women should be obligated, the mishna teaches that they are nevertheless exempt.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
How does Rava derive that women are obligated in kiddush by Torah law?
A. From the juxtaposition of kiddush and mezuza
B. From 'evening, morning, and afternoon' in Psalms
C. From 'Remember' and 'Observe': whoever is included in observing Shabbat is included in remembering it
D. From the fact that women are obligated in prayer
Correct: C — Rava rejected Abaye's claim that it is only rabbinic and derived it from the two versions of the commandment (Exodus 20:8 and Deuteronomy 5:12).
QUESTION 5 OF 5
How does the Gemara refute the proof from 'a son may bless for his father' that women's Grace after Meals is Torah law?
A. A minor is certainly not Torah-obligated, so the baraita must refer to a father who ate only a rabbinic measure
B. The baraita is only a curse, not a halakha
C. The son in the baraita is an adult
D. Women are never mentioned in the baraita
Correct: A — Since a minor is exempt by Torah law yet the baraita lets him bless for his father, the father must have eaten less than satiety, an amount obligating Grace only rabbinically — so rabbinic fulfills rabbinic, and the proof collapses.
