Berakhot 36 Quiz

QUESTION 1 OF 5
In what case does the Gemara finally locate Shmuel and Rabbi Yoḥanan's ruling that one recites 'Who creates fruit of the tree' over olive oil?
A. When one drinks the oil plain
B. When one dips bread into the oil
C. When one with a sore throat swallows a large amount of oil mixed into anigeron
D. When the oil is used to anoint the body
Correct: C — Drinking oil plain is harmful, and with bread the bread is primary. Only when a person with a sore throat adds much oil to anigeron is the oil primary, and despite the medicinal intent he blesses because he derives pleasure.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
Why does the Gemara reject Rava's comparison of wheat flour to olive oil?
A. Because flour is not eaten by anyone
B. Because oil is a finished product, while flour still has further enhancement ahead as bread
C. Because flour comes from the ground and oil from a tree
D. Because Rav Naḥman was Rava's teacher
Correct: B — Olive oil has no further enhancement, so it keeps the olive's blessing. Flour is a raw material on its way to bread, so neither the wheat blessing nor the bread blessing fits, and one says shehakol.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why is the halakha in accordance with Shmuel that one recites shehakol over the heart of palm, even though Shmuel praised Rav Yehuda's view?
A. Because the heart of palm is harmful like barley flour
B. Because it ultimately hardens, just like a radish
C. Because people do not plant palm trees with the heart of palm in mind, unlike radishes or caper leaves
D. Because Shmuel retracted his praise
Correct: C — The decisive test is what people plant for. A radish is planted for the soft radish and a caper for its leaves, but no one plants a palm for its heart, so it is not a fruit.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
What is Rava's final definition of a 'protection for the fruit' (shomer lapri) that is included in orla?
A. Anything that exists both while attached and after picking
B. Anything present when the fruit is fully ripe
C. Anything that joins the fruit for the laws of ritual impurity
D. Anything whose removal causes the fruit to die
Correct: D — The first two definitions were refuted by the pomegranate flower and the date coverings. Rava concludes that a shomer is that whose removal kills the fruit, confirmed by the experiment in which the pomegranate withered but the caper survived.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
How does the Gemara reconcile Rava's ruling that chewing peppers on Yom Kippur is exempt with Rabbi Meir's teaching that the pepper tree is a 'tree for food' obligated in orla?
A. Rabbi Meir refers to fresh peppers; Rava refers to dry peppers
B. Rabbi Meir refers to peppers in Eretz Yisrael only
C. Rava retracted in favor of Rav Sheshet
D. Rabbi Meir refers to the wood of the tree, not its fruit
Correct: A — Fresh, damp peppers are food and are obligated in orla; dry peppers are not considered eating, so chewing them on Yom Kippur is exempt and no blessing is recited. The same distinction resolves the question about ginger.
