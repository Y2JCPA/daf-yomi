Berakhot 3 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Why does Rabbi Eliezer say 'until the end of the first watch' rather than simply 'until four hours'?
A. Because he holds the night has four watches
B. To teach that there are watches in heaven just as there are watches on earth
C. Because people in his day could not count hours at night
D. Because the evening Shema may be recited until dawn
Correct: B — The Gemara concludes that Rabbi Eliezer does hold three watches, and used the language of 'watches' to teach that the night in the upper worlds is also divided into watches, over each of which God roars like a lion.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What three things did Rabbi Yosei learn from Elijah at the ruin?
A. One prays only in a synagogue; one must pray at length; one may enter a ruin if it is new
B. One does not enter a ruin; one may pray on the road; one who prays on the road recites an abbreviated prayer
C. One must greet a rabbi first; one waits for a companion; one prays facing Jerusalem
D. One prays three times daily; one answers 'May His great name be blessed'; one avoids travelers
Correct: B — Rabbi Yosei explicitly lists the three lessons: do not enter a ruin, one may pray along the road, and one who prays on the road says the short prayer so as to keep his focus.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
From which verse does Rabbi Natan derive that the night has three watches?
A. 'At midnight I rise to give thanks' (Psalms 119:62)
B. 'The Lord roars from on high' (Jeremiah 25:30)
C. 'Gideon came to the edge of the camp at the beginning of the middle watch' (Judges 7:19)
D. 'I rose with the neshef and cried' (Psalms 119:147)
Correct: C — A 'middle' watch implies exactly one before it and one after it, so the night must have three watches. Rabbi Yehuda HaNasi counters that 'middle' could mean one of the two middle watches.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
In which case is 'because of demons' the only reason not to enter a ruin?
A. Two upstanding people entering an old ruin in the city
B. A single person entering a new ruin located in a field
C. Two immoral people entering any ruin
D. A single person entering an old ruin near a road
Correct: B — In a new ruin there is no fear of collapse, and in a field a woman is unlikely to be found so there is no suspicion; only the concern of demons remains. Alternatively, two people in a place known to be haunted by demons.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
How did King David know when midnight had arrived?
A. Moses had taught him the exact calculation
B. The Sages of Israel came to wake him
C. He heard the donkey bray at the end of the first watch
D. A lyre hung over his bed and the north wind caused it to play at midnight
Correct: D — Rav Aḥa bar Bizna in the name of Rabbi Shimon Ḥasida: at midnight the northern wind blew through the lyre above David's bed and it played by itself, and David rose to study Torah until dawn.
