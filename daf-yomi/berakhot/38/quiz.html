Berakhot 38 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
Why does Mar bar Rav Ashi rule that one recites shehakol over date honey?
A. Because honey is never eaten alone
B. Because it is mere moisture that drips from the fruit, not the fruit itself
C. Because dates are not among the seven species
D. Because it is made only outside Eretz Yisrael
Correct: B — Date honey is 'mere sweat' of the fruit. The Gemara links this to Rabbi Yehoshua, who exempts one who ate teruma date honey or fruit juice from the added fifth because such juices are byproducts, not the fruit.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
How does Rav Ḥisda reconcile Rav (shehakol) and Shmuel (mezonot) regarding shetita?
A. Rav speaks of barley, Shmuel of wheat
B. Rav speaks of a thin mixture made as medicine, Shmuel of a thick one made as food
C. Rav speaks of Shabbat, Shmuel of weekdays
D. Rav retracted after Rav Yosef's objection
Correct: B — They do not disagree: a thick shetita is prepared as food and gets mezonot; a thin one is prepared as medicine and gets shehakol.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to the Gemara, why did the son of Rav Zevid recite motzi rather than hamotzi?
A. He held like Rabbi Neḥemya that hamotzi is present tense
B. He was not actually expert in blessings
C. He wished to remove himself from the dispute, since motzi is valid according to all
D. Rabbi Zeira had instructed him to do so
Correct: C — Rabbi Zeira was disappointed that the guest taught nothing new. The Gemara explains he deliberately chose the formula acceptable to everyone, so as not to take sides. The halakha nonetheless is hamotzi.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Which vegetables does Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak give as examples of foods that are ha'adama raw but shehakol when boiled, according to Rav Ḥisda's view?
A. Cabbage and chard
B. Pumpkin and lupin
C. Garlic and leeks
D. Radish and heart of palm
Correct: C — Rav Ḥisda's intermediate view says boiling can damage a vegetable normally eaten raw. Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak identifies garlic and leeks; cabbage, chard and pumpkin are the opposite case, improved by boiling.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What was one of Rabbi Zeira's reasons for preferring Rabbi Ḥiyya bar Abba's report of Rabbi Yoḥanan over Rabbi Binyamin bar Yefet's?
A. Rabbi Ḥiyya bar Abba reviewed his learning before Rabbi Yoḥanan every thirty days
B. Rabbi Ḥiyya bar Abba was older
C. Rabbi Binyamin bar Yefet lived in Babylonia
D. Rabbi Ḥiyya bar Abba was Rabbi Yoḥanan's son
Correct: A — Rabbi Zeira gave three grounds: Rabbi Ḥiyya bar Abba was meticulous, he reviewed his studies before Rabbi Yoḥanan every thirty days, and Rabbi Yoḥanan himself ruled ha'adama over a lupin boiled seven times.
