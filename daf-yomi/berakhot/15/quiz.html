Berakhot 15 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Rabbi Yoḥanan, one who relieves himself, washes his hands, dons tefillin, recites Shema and prays is credited as if he did what?
A. Studied the entire Torah
B. Built an altar and offered a sacrifice upon it
C. Fasted an entire day
D. Brought first fruits to the Temple
Correct: B — Rabbi Ḥiyya bar Abba cited Rabbi Yoḥanan: the verse 'I will wash in purity my hands, and I will encircle the altar of the Lord' (Psalms 26:6) credits him as if he built an altar and offered a sacrifice; Rava added that it is even as if he immersed his whole body.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
How did Rava justify the scholar from Eretz Yisrael who said one may wipe his hands with earth, a rock or wood when there is no water?
A. Washing hands before Shema is only a custom
B. The verse says 'I will wash in purity', not 'with water' — anything that cleans suffices
C. Earth is considered purer than water
D. The scholar was following Rabbi Yosei
Correct: B — Rava said he spoke well: the verse does not say 'I will wash with water' but 'in purity' (בְּנִקָּיוֹן), so anything that cleans is sufficient. Rav Ḥisda even cursed one who went searching for water at the time of prayer — though for prayer, unlike Shema, one must seek water up to a parsa ahead.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What is Rabbi Yosei's reason for requiring that Shema be audible to one's own ear?
A. Shema must be recited only in Hebrew
B. The word 'Shema' means: make your ear hear what your mouth utters
C. A blessing said silently is not a blessing
D. The verse 'Pay attention, and hear, Israel' refers to Shema
Correct: B — Rabbi Yosei reads 'Shema' literally — make your ears hear what your mouth utters. The first tanna uses the word to teach that Shema may be recited in any language one hears and understands; Rabbi Yosei derives both halakhot from it.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why did Rav Ḥisda, citing Rav Sheila, need to say both 'the halakha follows Rabbi Yehuda in the name of Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya' and 'the halakha follows Rabbi Yehuda'?
A. To rule that a deaf person may never separate teruma
B. The first alone might imply there is no remedy after the fact; the second alone might imply inaudible recitation is fine even ab initio
C. Because Rabbi Yehuda and Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya disagreed with each other
D. To reject Rabbi Yosei's view about enunciation as well
Correct: B — The Gemara explains both statements are needed: 'like Rabbi Yehuda' alone might mean permitted even ab initio, while 'must make it audible' alone might mean invalid after the fact. Together they teach: audible ab initio, but valid after the fact.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What reward does Rabbi Ḥama son of Rabbi Ḥanina promise one who is punctilious in enunciating the letters of Shema?
A. Gehenna is cooled for him
B. His prayers are answered that same day
C. He is credited as if he wrote a Torah scroll
D. His days are lengthened
Correct: A — Rabbi Ḥama reads Psalms 68:15 — 'When the Almighty scatters (befares) kings... it will snow in Tzalmon' — as befaresh (when he enunciates) and betzalmavet (the shadow of death, Gehenna): Gehenna is cooled for one who enunciates precisely.
