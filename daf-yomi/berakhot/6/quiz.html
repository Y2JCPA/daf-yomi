Berakhot 6 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
If the Shekhina rests even on two who study Torah, why does the Gemara need a separate verse for three who sit in judgment?
A. Because three constitute a congregation
B. Because judges must first immerse
C. Lest you think judgment merely keeps the peace and is not Torah — it teaches that judgment is also Torah
D. Because the Shekhina arrives before the judges do
Correct: C — The Gemara asks why three is needed after two, and answers that one might think judgment is not Torah study and does not attract the Shekhina; Ravin bar Rav Adda teaches that sitting in judgment is also Torah.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to Rav Ḥiyya bar Avin, what is written in the tefillin of the Master of the world?
A. 'Hear, Israel, the Lord is our God, the Lord is One'
B. 'Who is like Your people Israel, one nation in the land'
C. 'The Lord has sworn by His right hand'
D. 'From His right hand, a fiery law for His people'
Correct: B — Just as Israel declares God's oneness in Shema, God declares Israel's oneness in His tefillin with I Chronicles 17:21.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
How does Abaye qualify Rav Huna's statement that one who prays behind the synagogue is called wicked?
A. It applies only on Shabbat
B. It applies only if he does not turn his face toward the synagogue
C. It applies only when a minyan is praying inside
D. It applies only to one who prays aloud
Correct: B — Abaye limits the ruling to one who prays with his back to the synagogue; one who faces the synagogue while praying outside it is not faulted. The story of Elijah killing the man who did not face it reinforces the gravity.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Why did Rabbi Zeira change his mind about sages running to the lecture on Shabbat?
A. He learned that running is permitted only before dawn
B. He heard Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi's teaching that one should always run to a matter of halakha, even on Shabbat
C. He was told that the reward for the lecture is understanding, not attendance
D. He saw Elijah running to the study hall
Correct: B — Rabbi Zeira initially thought the running desecrated Shabbat, but on hearing Rabbi Tanḥum citing Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi — 'they shall walk after the Lord, who will roar like a lion' — he began to run as well.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to Rav Huna, what is one called who is greeted by another and does not return the greeting?
A. A robber
B. Wicked
C. A fool
D. One who lacks fear of Heaven
Correct: A — Rav Huna derives from 'the spoils of the poor are in your houses' (Isaiah 3:14) that withholding a greeting robs the other of the only thing a pauper has — his dignity.
