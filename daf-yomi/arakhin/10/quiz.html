Arakhin 10 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to the mishna, what is the minimum and maximum number of trumpet blasts sounded daily in the Temple?
A. A minimum of 7 and a maximum of 16
B. A minimum of 21 and a maximum of 48
C. A minimum of 2 and a maximum of 6
D. Always exactly 21, with no variation
Correct: B — The mishna sets the daily range at twenty-one blasts (for gate-opening and the two daily offerings) up to forty-eight on the Friday of Sukkot's water-drawing ceremony.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What is the essential disagreement between the Rabbis and Rabbi Yehuda over counting a series of shofar/trumpet blasts?
A. The Rabbis count tekiah, teruah, and tekiah as three separate units; Rabbi Yehuda counts the whole sequence as one unit
B. They disagree about which instrument should be used
C. The Rabbis require six blasts per day; Rabbi Yehuda requires none
D. They disagree about who is permitted to sound the blasts
Correct: A — This counting difference explains why the mishna's range (21–48) differs from Rabbi Yehuda's baraita (7–16) — the same physical sequence of sounds is simply tallied differently.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why is the full Hallel recited every day of Sukkot but only on the first day of Pesach?
A. Because Sukkot's additional offerings differ from day to day, while Pesach's remain the same each day
B. Because Pesach is considered less important than Sukkot
C. Because Hallel may only be recited in the fall
D. Because Sukkot lasts longer, so more Hallel is required by default
Correct: A — The Gemara explains that each day of Sukkot has a distinct set of additional offerings (the bull count changes daily), making each day unique and Hallel-worthy, while Pesach's offerings are identical throughout.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
According to Rabbi Abbahu, why is Hallel not recited on Rosh HaShana and Yom Kippur, even though both qualify as sanctified festivals?
A. Because these days fall in the autumn, when Hallel is never said
B. The ministering angels asked why Israel doesn't sing before God on these days, and He answered that joyous song is inappropriate while the King sits in judgment with the books of life and death open
C. Because no offerings are brought on those days
D. Because Rosh HaShana and Yom Kippur are not mentioned in the Torah
Correct: B — Rabbi Abbahu relates this exchange between the angels and God as the reason these solemn Days of Judgment lack the joyous recitation of Hallel.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What happened when the Temple's reed flute was plated with gold, according to the baraita?
A. Its sound became even more pleasant
B. Its pleasant sound was ruined; removing the gold plating restored the original sweet sound
C. It shattered and had to be replaced entirely
D. Nothing changed — the plating had no effect
Correct: B — The baraita reports that gold-plating the flute by royal command spoiled its sound, and only removing the plating brought back its original pleasant tone.
