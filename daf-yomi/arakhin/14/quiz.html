Arakhin 14 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
What does the Gemara say the mishna teaches 'in passing' by describing only 'the most beautiful of Israel' rather than contrasting a Jew with a gentile?
A. That gentiles have no market value at all
B. That it is forbidden for a person to say, 'How beautiful is this gentile'
C. That gentiles cannot be valuated under any circumstances
D. That the mishna was originally written differently
Correct: B — Rav Yehuda in the name of Rav derives this prohibition from the mishna's choice to speak only within one nation rather than contrasting Jew and gentile.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to the mishna's first opinion, what is the key difference between redeeming an ancestral field and a purchased field?
A. An ancestral field is always redeemed at the Torah's flat rate of 50 shekels regardless of quality; a purchased field is redeemed at its actual market value
B. Both are always redeemed at exactly the same rate
C. A purchased field can never be redeemed at all
D. Only a purchased field requires an additional fifth
Correct: A — The mishna's leniency/stringency example shows the ancestral field's flat rate applies to both poor and prime land, while the purchased field tracks real market value.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
According to Rav Pappa, why is rocky, unsowable land (terashin) redeemed at its actual value rather than the Torah's flat rate?
A. Because rocky land has no halakhic status at all
B. Because the verse specifies redemption for land 'fit for sowing,' which excludes rocky land unsuitable for planting
C. Because rocky land is automatically exempt from consecration
D. Because only purchased fields can be rocky
Correct: B — Rav Pappa derives this from the verse's requirement of an area 'fit for sowing,' which rocky, unplantable land does not satisfy.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
According to the mishna, what fixed fine does a forewarned ox's owner pay for killing a Canaanite slave, regardless of the slave's actual worth?
A. 50 sela
B. 30 sela
C. The slave's exact market value
D. No fixed fine — it always varies
Correct: B — The mishna sets a flat fine of 30 sela (per Exodus 21:32) whether the slave killed was the most valuable or the least valuable among slaves.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to Rav Zeira's challenge, why can't the rapist or seducer's fifty-sela fine be the entire payment owed?
A. Because fifty sela is too small a sum to ever be paid
B. Because it would be absurd for people to say that violating a king's daughter and an ordinary woman results in exactly the same total payment — degradation must be assessed separately based on who was involved
C. Because the Torah requires a different fine for each tribe
D. Because the fine only applies to unmarried women
Correct: B — Rav Zeira argues that treating the fixed fine as covering everything would produce an absurd result, since it would equate the disgrace of a king's daughter with that of an ordinary woman — so humiliation and degradation must be compensated separately.
