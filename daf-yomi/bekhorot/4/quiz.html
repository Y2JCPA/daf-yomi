Bekhorot 4 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to Rava, what does the mishna's a fortiori (kal vachomer) argument actually prove?
A. That the animals of priests and Levites have a special exempting power
B. That priests and Levites exempt themselves, since the Levites' sanctity abrogated Israel's firstborn sanctity in the wilderness
C. That only kosher animals of Levites are exempt
D. That the argument proves nothing and is rejected
Correct: B — Rava rejects Abaye's animal-based reading (which would wrongly also exempt kosher firstborn animals) and explains the mishna means the priests and Levites exempted themselves.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
From where does the Gemara derive that priests, not just Levites by name, are included in this exemption?
A. From a direct verse naming priests
B. From Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi's statement that priests are called 'Levites' in 24 biblical places, including Ezekiel 44:15
C. From an oral tradition with no verse
D. Priests are not actually included
Correct: B — Rabbi Yehoshua ben Levi notes that priests are called 'Levites' in numerous verses, including Ezekiel 44:15, so any halakha about Levites applies to priests too.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
How does the Gemara answer Rav Safra's challenge that a Levite woman's firstborn son (married to an Israelite) should not be exempt, since women weren't counted as substitutes?
A. It concedes the son is obligated
B. It explains that firstborn status depends on 'firstborn of the womb' — the mother's Levite status alone exempts him
C. It says only firstborn daughters are affected
D. It cites a different unrelated verse
Correct: B — Since the Torah ties firstborn status to being the firstborn of the womb (the mother), a Levite mother's status is sufficient to exempt her son, regardless of the wilderness census.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
What textual proof does the Gemara bring that a single Levite's lamb could exempt many Israelite firstborn donkeys?
A. The Torah explicitly counts a large surplus of donkeys
B. The verse uses 'behemat' (singular) for the Levites but 'behemtam' (plural) for the Israelites
C. A later rabbi simply asserted it without proof
D. The Torah never discusses donkey redemption
Correct: B — The grammatical mismatch — singular for the Levites' animal, plural for Israel's animals — indicates one Levite animal covered many Israelite firstborn donkeys.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
What is Reish Lakish's position on whether firstborns born in the wilderness (after the Exodus but before entering the land) were sanctified?
A. They were sanctified just like those born in Egypt
B. They were not sanctified, based on the verse 'when the Lord brings you into the land...you shall set apart all firstborn'
C. Only firstborn animals were sanctified, not firstborn sons
D. The question never arose in the wilderness
Correct: B — Reish Lakish infers from Exodus 13:11–12 that firstborn sanctity was tied to entry into the land, implying that those born in the wilderness beforehand were not sanctified.
