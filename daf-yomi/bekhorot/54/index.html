📖 Overview: Deriving the Rules of Separate Tithing

Bekhorot 54a–54b
מַעְשַׂר בְּהֵמָה מִצְטָרֵף כִּמְלֹא רֶגֶל בְּהֵמָה רוֹעָה
The scene: The daf continues building, through a chain of a fortiori inferences, the principle that different species or categories of produce and animals cannot be tithed for one another — wine cannot cover grain, cattle cannot cover flock, and old-year animals cannot cover new-year ones. Rava eventually retracts an earlier position and settles the matter through the verse's own juxtapositions. The daf then turns to a new mishna: animals scattered across a distance join together for tithing purposes only within the reach of a single shepherd's eye — sixteen mil — and the Gemara works out, through Rav and Shmuel's dispute, exactly how many animals in the middle bridge a wider gap.

🍇 Building the Chain: No Species Tithes Another

From wine-and-oil to grain-and-grain by a fortiori reasoning
1️⃣ Established: wine and oil cannot be tithed for each other (from 'give the best of this, and of that')
↓
2️⃣ A fortiori: if wine/oil — not even subject to diverse-kinds — still can't combine, surely wine/grain or grain/grain, which ARE diverse kinds, cannot combine either
↓
3️⃣ Rabbi Yoshiya's harder case: he requires a single-handed sowing of wheat, barley, AND grape seed together for the diverse-kinds prohibition to apply at all — so the simple a fortiori doesn't work for him
↓
4️⃣ His alternative: wine/oil aren't diverse kinds EVEN WITH added seeds; grain pairs ARE diverse kinds when combined with something else — so the a fortiori still holds, just via a different comparison

🐐 Cattle vs. Flock, and Sheep vs. Goats

Abaye and Rava dispute the textual source
Question
The verse doesn't say 'tithe of cattle AND tithe of flock' twice — so why can't they be tithed together, the way sheep and goats can?
Abaye
'The TENTH shall be sacred' — give the tenth from THIS one, and the tenth from THAT one, separately. Sheep and goats combine only because 'or flock' groups them as one category.
Rava (first view)
No verse is even needed — animal tithe is juxtaposed to grain tithe: just as different grains don't combine, different animal species don't combine either.
Rava (retracted)
I later retracted that — the juxtaposition to grain tithe was only about NEW vs. OLD flocks ('year by year'), not about species in general. (Some attribute this retraction to Rav Pappa instead.)

🚶 How Far Can a Shepherd's Flock Spread?

The new mishna's sixteen-mil rule
📜 Mishna: animals join for tithing within the span a single shepherd can walk and still tend them — 16 mil
↓
📖 Source: Jeremiah 33:12–13, 'the flock shall again pass under the hands of him who counts them' — one shepherd's flock is one unit
↓
👁️ Tradition: a shepherd's eye reaches sixteen mil — that becomes the halakhic maximum distance
↓
❌ Beyond 32 mil total (16+16), the two groups do NOT combine at all

🌉 Bridging the Gap: Rav vs. Shmuel

How many animals in the middle unite two distant flocks?

Rav's View

  • Five animals on each end plus five in the middle
  • The middle five can combine with either side to reach ten
  • That connects both distant flocks into one unit

Shmuel's View

  • Even just ONE animal in the middle suffices
  • We view the shepherd as though standing there himself
  • The verse's 'counter' (moneh) applies to him at that midpoint
