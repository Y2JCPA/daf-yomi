📖 Overview: The Choreography of the Daily Offering

Tamid 30a–30b
צְאוּ וּרְאוּ אִם הִגִּיעַ זְמַן שְׁחִיטָה
The scene: After a brief dispute about why the wood arrangement had an opening (for wind, or for lighting kindling), the mishna moves into the second of the Temple's four daily lotteries — assigning thirteen distinct tasks to thirteen priests, from slaughtering to carrying each limb and vessel up to the altar. The mishna then tracks the sunrise announcement, the fetching and torchlit re-examination of the lamb, and an astonishing list of Temple sounds and smells reportedly perceptible all the way in Jericho. It closes with the precise, almost architectural choreography of opening the Sanctuary's gates, removing ash from the inner altar and Candelabrum, and finally binding, slaughtering, and sprinkling the blood of the daily lamb itself.

📢 A Temple So Grand, Jericho Could Hear and Smell It

Dawn's declaration, and an extraordinary list of sounds
בַּרְקַאי
The scene: At the appointed moment, a priest goes out to check the sky; if dawn has broken (either simply declaring 'Barkai!' — 'It has lit up!' — or, per Matya ben Shmuel, confirming light as far as Hebron), the day's service may begin. The mishna then lists sounds reportedly audible all the way in Jericho: the Great Gate opening, the ash-shovel's clang, the Levites' song, ben Arza's cymbal crash, the flute (played 12 days a year), Gevini the crier's call to duty, the creak of ben Katin's Basin-mechanism, the shofar, and — some say — even the High Priest's voice pronouncing God's name on Yom Kippur. The incense's fragrance reportedly reached so far that, per Rabbi Elazar ben Diglai, his father's goats grazing near Mikhvar would sneeze from its scent.

1️⃣ The officer sends priests to the Chamber of the Lambs (northwest corner of the Chamber of the Hearth building) — one of four chambers there, alongside the Chamber of Seals, the Hearth itself, and the shewbread-preparation chamber
↓
2️⃣ Priests enter the Chamber of Vessels and remove 93 gold and silver service vessels needed for the day's service
↓
3️⃣ The selected lamb is given water to drink from an actual gold cup — though it was already checked the previous evening
↓
4️⃣ It is re-examined now, in the dark, by torchlight — leaving nothing to chance before the offering begins

🔑 Through the Forever-Northern Wicket

Entering the Sanctuary to open the Great Gate and remove the inner ashes
🔑 The priest assigned to inner-altar/Candelabrum ash removal carries a basket, a jug, and two keys — heading in ahead of the others
↓
🚪 He reaches the NORTHERN wicket of the Great Gate — the SOUTHERN one is never used by anyone, per Ezekiel's prophecy that it stays permanently sealed since God entered through it (Ezekiel 44:1–2)
↓
🗝️ He unlocks the wicket, passes through a compartment into the Sanctuary, then reaches and opens the Great Gate itself, moving its bolt and locks
↓
🔪 Only once the priests outside HEAR the Great Gate open does the slaughterer proceed to slaughter the daily lamb
↓
🧹 Inside: the ash-removal priests empty the inner altar into the basket, and tend the Candelabrum's lamps — refueling extinguished ones from the two still-burning eastern lamps, or from the outer altar's fire if all had gone out
