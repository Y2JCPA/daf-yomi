QUESTION 1 OF 5
How many distinct tasks were assigned in the second daily lottery, and what determined who performed the slaughter?
A. Five tasks; the oldest priest present slaughtered
B. Thirteen tasks; whoever won the lottery performed the slaughter, with the twelve priests to his right receiving the remaining tasks
C. Two tasks; determined by seniority
D. Thirteen tasks, but slaughter was always performed by the High Priest
Correct: B — The mishna describes thirteen tasks (slaughter, sprinkling, two ash-removals, and nine carrying roles), with the lottery winner slaughtering and the twelve priests to his right receiving the other assignments in order.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
According to the mishna's list, what sounds and scents were reportedly perceptible in Jericho from the Temple?
A. Only the sound of the shofar
B. The Great Gate opening, the ash-shovel's clang, Levite song, cymbals, the flute, the crier's call, ben Katin's mechanism, the shofar, and the fragrance of the incense
C. Only the High Priest's voice
D. Nothing was audible from Jericho; this is a later legend not in the mishna
Correct: B — The mishna lists a striking series of sounds (gate, shovel, song, cymbals, flute, crier, mechanism, shofar) and even the incense's fragrance as perceptible from Jericho.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why does the priest opening the Great Gate use the NORTHERN wicket rather than the southern one?
A. The southern wicket didn't exist
B. The southern wicket was permanently sealed, per Ezekiel's prophecy that no one may enter through the gate God Himself entered by (Ezekiel 44:1–2)
C. Only the High Priest could use the southern wicket
D. The northern wicket was simply closer to the altar
Correct: B — The mishna explains that the southern wicket was never used by anyone, based on Ezekiel's prophecy that this specific gate remains shut since God entered through it.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
At what point does the priest slaughter the daily lamb, according to the mishna?
A. Immediately after the lamb is fetched from its chamber
B. Only after he hears that the Great Gate has been opened
C. Before dawn, while it is still dark
D. Only after the incense is burned
Correct: B — The mishna specifies that the slaughterer waits to hear the sound of the Great Gate being opened before proceeding with the slaughter.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
How was the daily lamb bound before slaughter, and where exactly was the morning offering slaughtered?
A. All four legs tied together; slaughtered anywhere on the altar's north side
B. Each hind leg bound to its corresponding foreleg (not all four together); slaughtered at the northwest corner of the altar, at the second of 24 floor rings
C. The lamb was not bound at all; slaughtered at the southern corner
D. Bound with rope around its neck only; slaughtered at the entrance to the Sanctuary
Correct: B — The mishna specifies a particular binding method (hind leg to foreleg) and precise slaughter locations — the morning offering at the northwest corner's second ring, the afternoon offering at the northeast corner's second ring.
