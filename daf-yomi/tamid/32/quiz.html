Tamid 32 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to the Elders of the Negev's answer to Alexander, who is considered truly wise?
A. The one who has the most books
B. The one who sees and anticipates the consequences of his behavior (הָרוֹאֶה אֶת הַנּוֹלָד)
C. The one who wins every argument
D. The one who has traveled the most
Correct: B — The Elders answer with the classic teaching that true wisdom lies in foreseeing the consequences of one's actions, a formulation also found in Pirkei Avot.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What happened when Alexander asked the women's town for bread and they served him bread made of gold?
A. He was pleased and stayed there
B. He questioned why they gave him inedible gold bread, and they responded that he must have really come for wealth, not food, since he had bread at home
C. He declared war immediately
D. He refused to eat anything they offered
Correct: B — The women's pointed response taught Alexander that his campaign was truly about seeking wealth, not sustenance, since he already had bread available at home.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
What lesson did Alexander learn from the eyeball he received at the gate of the Garden of Eden?
A. That gold is more valuable than anything else
B. That a mortal eye is never satisfied while it can see — demonstrated when covering it with a small amount of dirt balanced the scale against all his treasure
C. That eyeballs are physically heavier than gold
D. That he should return all his wealth
Correct: B — The Sages explained that the eyeball represents human desire, endlessly unsatisfied while sight remains — a lesson drawn from Proverbs 27:20 about the eyes never being satiated.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
According to Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya, how do Torah scholars 'increase peace in the world,' based on Isaiah 54:13?
A. By avoiding conflict entirely
B. By reading 'your children' (בָּנַיִךְ) as 'your builders' (בּוֹנַיִךְ) — scholars literally build and increase peace through their study
C. By serving as judges in every dispute
D. By teaching only in times of war
Correct: B — Rabbi Elazar ben Azarya's teaching plays on the similar Hebrew words for 'children' and 'builders,' suggesting Torah scholars actively construct peace in the world.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
According to the new mishna, who was eligible to participate in the third lottery, for burning the incense?
A. Only the High Priest
B. Only priests who had never before burned the incense — a once-in-a-lifetime honor
C. Only the most senior priests
D. Any priest present, regardless of prior experience
Correct: B — The mishna specifies that only new priests — those who had never previously performed the incense service — were called to participate in this particular lottery.
