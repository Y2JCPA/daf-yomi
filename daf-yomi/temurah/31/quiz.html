Temurah 31 Quiz

5 questions · Self-graded · Test your learning!
QUESTION 1 OF 5
According to the view that a tereifa CAN give birth, where do Rabbi Eliezer and the Rabbis disagree?
A. Where the animal conceived first and then became a tereifa
B. Where the animal became a tereifa first and then conceived
C. Where the animal was consecrated before becoming a tereifa
D. Where the offspring itself became a tereifa
Correct: B — On that premise the case is tereifa-then-pregnant, and the dispute is the general rule of 'this permitted factor and that prohibited factor': Rabbi Eliezer bars the result, the Rabbis permit it like its father.
QUESTION 2 OF 5
What is Abaye's objection to Rav Huna's ruling about a chick from a tereifa's egg?
A. A hen cannot become a tereifa
B. A chick emerges only once the egg has rotted, and what has rotted is mere dust no longer ascribed to the hen
C. Eggs are always permitted since they are not flesh
D. Rav Huna contradicted his own earlier ruling
Correct: B — Abaye argues the reverse of Rava's reading and adds a decisive point: a baraita explicitly states that Rabbi Eliezer concedes the chick from a tereifa's egg may be sacrificed, contrary to Rav Huna.
QUESTION 3 OF 5
Why is a kosher animal that suckled from a tereifa disqualified, according to Rabbi Ḥanina Terita's teaching before Rabbi Yoḥanan?
A. Because any nourishment from a tereifa transfers its status
B. Because it was given warm milk each morning and so survives entirely on the tereifa for each twenty-four-hour period
C. Because the milk of a tereifa is itself a sacrificial by-product
D. Because the animal is presumed to have become a tereifa itself
Correct: B — Mere fattening cannot be the reason — feeding an animal vetches of idolatry does not bar it. The disqualification requires that the animal's continued existence depend on the tereifa.
QUESTION 4 OF 5
Which of these is true of animals consecrated for the altar but NOT of property consecrated for Temple maintenance?
A. Unspecified consecrations default to it
B. One is liable for misuse of its by-products such as milk and eggs
C. It renders an animal exchanged for it a substitute
D. The Temple treasurer may pay craftsmen from it
Correct: C — Only altar consecrations generate temurah. The other three options are all features of Temple-maintenance consecrations, which the mishna lists in the opposite column.
QUESTION 5 OF 5
From where does Rabbi Abbahu derive that craftsmen may be paid from funds consecrated for Temple maintenance?
A. 'You may slaughter and eat flesh within all your gates' (Deut. 12:15)
B. 'And let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them' (Ex. 25:8)
C. 'The hope of man is a worm' (Job 25:6)
D. 'You shall not bring the payment of a prostitute' (Deut. 23:19)
Correct: B — 'And let them make Me a sanctuary' indicates the Tabernacle may be constructed from that which is God's — from consecrated property — so maintenance funds may compensate the workers.
